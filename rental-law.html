Law by jurisdiction

Local SF Rent Ordinance, state AB 1482 / Civil Code, and federal Fair Housing Act — every row source-linked.

Research JSONResearch only
The lower cap controls.

Local SF rent control (1.6% AGA) governs these 1940 units; the state AB 1482 cap (8.8% for the SF Area, starts 8/1/2026-7/31/2027) applies only where no stricter local rule exists; federal law adds anti-discrimination protection, not a rent cap.
