Uncertainty & scope

10,000 paired circular moving-block resamples, five-game blocks, seed 20261103; percentile 95% intervals. Within-game pairs stay aligned; the finite block horizon is an assumption.

These exploratory intervals are not adjusted for selecting the player, dates, thresholds or comparisons. No formal significance or edge claim follows. A constant series produces a blank coefficient—not zero.

Actual multi-leg strategy validation additionally needs conditional pregame-model residuals, later joint calibration and an executable operator quote.

Thresholds are hypothetical project choices, not sportsbook lines. Joint rates are empirical frequencies, not forecast probabilities. A dependence difference does not establish a price advantage.

Use the right identity

P(A ∩ B) = p_A p_B + φ √[p_A(1−p_A)p_B(1−p_B)]

This identity applies to two Bernoulli hit indicators with their matching marginals. Check max(0, p_A+p_B−1) ≤ joint ≤ min(p_A,p_B). Do not insert a continuous points/assists correlation into this formula. Pairwise correlations do not identify a three-or-more-leg joint distribution. Zero continuous correlation does not imply independence.
