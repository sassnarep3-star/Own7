M04 · Player participation

TOTO online · NL · 28 Apr 2026 rules

The basketball player-market section says no participation makes the selection void; entering at any point makes it valid regardless of playing duration or the selected period. Its example covers a second-half player prop after first-half-only participation.

Research implication: Use actual game participation and the applicable period rule, rather than treating every non-start or early injury as a void.

M12 · Pushes

Fanatics · Ohio · 17 Sep 2026 rules

For two-way basketball spread and total markets, the rules state that a whole-number line matched exactly is a push and the market is voided.

Research implication: Keep push/refund outcomes separate from wins and losses, and do not apply a two-way rule to a three-way market.
