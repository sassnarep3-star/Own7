Market lifecycle — snapshots, order, signal, staking, demotion

Captures opening and closing numbers for every market this project originates, publishes the ordered pipeline (side → total → moneyline) and the fair / market / difference columns, separates leaderboard signal from staking, pre-registers the demotion gate, and tracks replacement depth. Every value carries an official source link; blanks are preserved and explained. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL; not a betting service.
