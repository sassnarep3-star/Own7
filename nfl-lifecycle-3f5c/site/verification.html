Method

Verification means: (1) the value exists verbatim in saved evidence transcribed from the official page on the read date; (2) arithmetic inside the publisher's own row is consistent (PCT half-up to three decimals — the publisher's convention — and NetPts = PF − PA); (3) cross-vintage moves are explained by exactly one officially published result; (4) derived values reconcile with the canonical engine. A value that cannot pass is left blank and flagged, never estimated.
