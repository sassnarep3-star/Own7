Why two tables

rank_by_signal() reads only SIGNAL_FIELDS; the staking table has no rank and no path into one a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal because staking outcomes are not part of the ranking computation at all

Tiers are inherited verbatim from the results engine (nfl-results/analysis.json). This desk does not re-grade anything; it republishes the graded ledger ranked on signal only, with the staking record beside it — not under it, and not feeding it.
