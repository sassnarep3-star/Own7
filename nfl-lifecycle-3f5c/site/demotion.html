Market lifecycle — snapshots, order, signal, staking, demotion

Captures opening and closing numbers for every market this project originates, publishes the ordered pipeline (side → total → moneyline) and the fair / market / difference columns, separates leaderboard signal from staking, pre-registers the demotion gate, and tracks replacement depth. Every value carries an official source link; blanks are preserved and explained. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL; not a betting service.

Pre-registered triggers (fixed 2026-11-14; not tunables)

Triggers are evaluated in order after every graded round while a strategy is live; the first firing trigger is recorded with its window. A demoted strategy re-enters the full promotion path as a new counted trial.
TriggerNameRuleEffect
D1Signal collapseOne-sided binomial P(X <= observed wins | n window, declared baseline) <= 0.01 over the last 20 graded selections (minimum 10 to evaluate).automatic demotion to paperREPO ↗
D2Repeated failureTwo consecutive windows both evaluate at P <= 0.05 against the declared baseline; fires even when neither window alone reached the D1 threshold.automatic demotion to paperREPO ↗
D3Calibration driftWindow mean Brier exceeds the promotion-time Brier by 0.05 or more (0.25 is the no-information reference).automatic demotion to paperREPO ↗
D4Integrity breachAny settlement number in the graded record is not traceable to a registered official source.immediate demotion; affected selections quarantined pending re-verificationREPO ↗
D5Governance breachAny stake placed outside the pre-registered staking plan recorded at promotion.immediate demotion; plan must be re-registered before re-promotionREPO ↗
