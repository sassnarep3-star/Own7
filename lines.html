Probability lab

A small, reproducible chronological demonstration. Calculated probabilities—not official forecasts, market lines or evidence of skill.

Research JSONResearch only
Retrospective probability demo—not a live line.

Fixed illustrative thresholds and one selected historical player. No actual pregame timestamps, untouched test, sportsbook lines or participation forecast. NBA-derived betting prices are withheld by the source-rights gate.

Review limitations →

A deliberately simple baseline

For each event: take only games before the target date, count threshold hits, and use a Beta(1,1) prior. Start after ten warmup games. A combo uses paired joint hits, never a product of single-leg probabilities.

p_demo = (earlier hits + 1) / (earlier games + 2)

Thresholds: PTS > 27.5, REB > 12.5, AST > 10.5; combo PTS > 27.5 and AST > 10.5. These are project choices, not verified offered lines or pre-registered selections.

Read these results conservatively

Each event has 22 chronological demonstrations, not 88 independent games. Lower scores against neutral p=0.5 do not establish skill: a rare combo can beat that baseline by its base rate alone.

No model tuning, market-relative skill, ROI, CLV or confirmatory p-value is claimed. Probabilities are calculated demonstration outputs, not official NBA values. Prices stay blank.
