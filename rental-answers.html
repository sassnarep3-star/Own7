What is a reasonable and normal rental increase for these units (94122) under local, state, and federal law?Q1Local (controls): the SF Rent Board annual allowable increase — 1.6% for effective dates 3/1/2026-2/28/2027 (60% of Bay Area CPI-U) — once per 12 months. Unit 1: +$63.92 to $4,058.92; Unit 2: +$50.40 to $3,200.40. State: AB 1482 caps at 8.8% for the SF Area for increases starting 8/1/2026-7/31/2027, but the lower local cap governs these rent-controlled units. Federal: no rent cap; only anti-discrimination rules. Anything above the local AGA needs a Rent Board petition.SF-NEWS-AGA ↗SF-RATES ↗CA-AG-RENTCAPS ↗LEGINFO-CIV1947-12 ↗GOVINFO-FHA ↗
How do big real estate corporations handle increases vs. individual owners in SF?Q2The same ordinances apply to every owner regardless of size — the SF Rent Ordinance and AB 1482 do not distinguish corporate from individual landlords (AB 1482's only ownership-based exemption is for non-corporate single-family homes). Both must hold a rent increase license, follow the AGA, and give proper notice. Claims about differing internal market practices of large firms are not verifiable from official sources — see flag R-F4.CA-AG-TENANTS ↗SF-RENTAL-LAWS ↗SF-HI-LICENSE ↗
What is the correct way to inform tenants of an increase, and what are reasonable reasons?Q3Written notice at least 30 days before the effective date (90 days if >10% combined in the year; +5 days if mailed), stating the dollar amount, percentage, effective date and any banked portion, using the rate in effect on the effective date, with a current rent increase license on file. The standard, reasonable reason is the annual CPI-based allowable increase; above-cap amounts require a Rent Board petition (capital improvements / operating-cost passthroughs).SF-RENT-INC ↗SF-BANKED ↗LEGINFO-CIV827 ↗SF-HI-LICENSE ↗
Will the increase survive scrutiny by SF tenant advocates / free tenant lawyers?Q4It is designed to: 1.6% is the published allowable increase, notice and licensing rules are followed, and both units are treated identically. Tenants have free resources — Rent Board counseling (415-252-4600) and Prop F Right to Counsel via the Eviction Defense Collaborative. Have a SF tenant-rights attorney review the actual notice before serving; this site is not legal advice.SF-NEWS-AGA ↗SF-RENT-INC ↗SF-HI-LICENSE ↗SF-RENTAL-LAWS ↗SF-RTC-EDC ↗
Raise one unit or both — discrimination risk?Q5Raise both units with the same lawful percentage and identical procedure. The federal Fair Housing Act (§3604(b)) prohibits discrimination in the terms and conditions of rental; singling out one unit without a documented, lawful, uniformly applied reason creates disparate-treatment risk. Uniform application is the defensible normal business practice.GOVINFO-FHA ↗HUD-FHEO ↗SF-RENT-INC ↗
