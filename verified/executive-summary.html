Executive Summary

Status as of 2026-11-03. Plain-language summary. Every number below is computed from a verified table on this site.

1. What was asked

  • Build NBA betting strategies (single bets, parlays, player-prop combinations) and rank them as a skill-based leaderboard.
  • Each strategy must pass validation gates. Correlation values must be determined. Luck should be separated from skill.
  • Repository review, a GitHub Pages site, and line-by-line checks against official sources. Anything that cannot be confirmed is left blank and flagged.

2. What is verified (official nba.com tables)

  • 2025-26 teams (30 rows): best record Oklahoma City Thunder 64–18 (.780); worst Washington Wizards 17–65 (.207). Teams
  • Points per game: highest Denver Nuggets 122.1; lowest Brooklyn Nets 105.9.
  • Field-goal %: highest Los Angeles Lakers 50.2%. Three-point %: highest Denver Nuggets 39.6%.
  • Point differential per game: highest Oklahoma City Thunder 11.1.
  • 2025-26 players (top 15 by PPG): highest scorer Luka Dončić (Los Angeles Lakers) 33.5 PPG in 64 games. Players
  • 2026-27 standings: 5–8 games per team so far. Leaders: Philadelphia 76ers (7–1, East) and Houston Rockets (7–1, West). Too few games to be a signal. Standings
  • Correlations (descriptive): across 30 teams, win % and point differential correlate at r = +0.973; win % and points per game at r = +0.632. These are season-level, not game-level. Correlation

3. What is not verified

  • No betting lines. nba.com publishes no odds. Spreads, totals, moneylines and prop lines are blank.
  • No leg-level correlation. Correlation between two bets in the same game needs game-level logs. The verified tables are season averages only.
  • Reported NBA restrictions (Oct 16, 2026) are media reports, not an official NBA document, and they cover US operators. See Betting Rules.
  • Netherlands: the Dutch regulator is the Ksa. Which licensed Dutch operators offer NBA markets is not verified. See Netherlands.

4. Strategy status

  • 0 of 8 strategies tested. 0 pass the validation gates. The leaderboard is empty, and no rewards are awarded (Leaderboard).
  • Assumption to test, not a verified fact: sportsbook prices include a margin, so most strategies may fail the gates. Any positive result would need out-of-sample, closing-line and multiple-testing evidence first.
