Executive Summary

Status as of 2026-11-03. Plain-language summary for review.

1. What was asked

  • Research and develop NBA betting strategies (single bets, parlays, player prop combinations), treated as a leaderboard competition.
  • Each strategy must pass validation gates, determine correlation values, and separate skill from luck.
  • Repo review, a GitHub Pages site, and line-by-line verification against official sources. Unverifiable values left blank and flagged.

2. What was delivered

  • A verified source registry with 7 official sources confirmed (Sources).
  • A strategy register of 8 hypotheses. None has been tested (Strategies).
  • Nine validation gates, the correlation method, and an empty leaderboard with ranking rules (Gates, Correlation, Leaderboard).
  • Repo review with 12 checks, including irregularities (Repo Review).

3. Key findings (verified)

  • Play Alberta, the AGLC-run platform, shows an NBA betting tile (playalberta.ca ↗).
  • Alberta iGaming operators may conduct registered activity from July 13, 2026. Registration and a commercial agreement are required (aglc.ca ↗).
  • Registered iGaming advertising must reference responsible-gambling information, including 211 Alberta (aglc.ca ↗).
  • Official helplines are confirmed: GameSense 1-833-447-7523; Self-Exclusion 1-844-468-8034; Addiction Helpline 1-866-332-2322 (details).

4. Reported but NOT verified

  • Media reports (October 16, 2026) say the NBA told sportsbooks to stop several bet types, including parlays with the under on the same player's stats, opening-tip winners, and unders for players expected to play under 12 minutes. No official NBA document was found, so these are flagged, not confirmed (Betting Rules).

5. Limits of this work

  • No official NBA numeric data. The NBA stats and data endpoints did not return data from this environment. Correlation values and strategy metrics are blank.
  • No odds or closing-line data. Without verified market prices, line origination and CLV tests cannot be run. Nothing here is a line or a pick.
  • No edge is shown. No strategy has passed a single gate. Assumption to test, not a verified fact: sportsbook prices include a margin, so most strategies may fail the gates. A positive result would need out-of-sample, CLV, and multiple-testing evidence first.
  • Rewards undefined. The brief asks for rewards for top strategies. No amount or funding is defined, and none is inferred (F-05).

6. Decisions for the owner (flagged, not blocking)

  • Provide an approved data path for official NBA data (for example, an accessible stats export), so the data gates can be run.
  • Add a license (none exists) and confirm reuse terms.
  • Confirm the conflicting Alberta statement (F-03) and the unverified helpline (F-04).

7. Responsible gambling

Set limits before any real money is involved. Treat any betting as a cost, not income. If gambling is affecting you or someone you know, see Responsible Gambling.
