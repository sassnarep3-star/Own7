Categories

  • Single bet: one game outcome (moneyline, spread, total).
  • Parlay: two or more outcomes combined. The payout multiplies, so errors in correlation assumptions compound.
  • Player prop: one player's statistic against a line.
  • Prop combo: several props combined. Leg correlation must be estimated (see Correlation).

Skill vs. luck

A strategy is "selectively skill based" only if it passes the gates on out-of-sample data, after pricing against a market baseline and correcting for the number of strategies tested. A short run of wins is not evidence of skill.
