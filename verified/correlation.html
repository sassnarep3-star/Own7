Method (for when game-level data is verified)

  • Use only official box-score rows. Measure within a single game where the legs share the game.
  • Report sample size and a confidence interval with each value. Never report a point estimate alone.
  • Compare the joint probability of a combo with the product of its legs. The gap is the correlation effect. The sportsbook may already price it.
