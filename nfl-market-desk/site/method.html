Method & limits

The eight limits that travel with every number, how to reproduce the figures, and what would change the conclusion.

Limits that travel with every number

  1. One provider, relayed. All prices are DraftKings as republished by ESPN. A movement is a movement in that one series, not a market-wide fact, and never an operator-confirmed fill.
  2. 15 games. Every rate is a description of fifteen games. Detecting a 55% rate at 80% power needs 783 settled bets.
  3. Stated priors, not fitted parameters. Margin and total standard deviations are registered choices. They were not tuned on the graded weeks, because tuning on the graded weeks is lookahead.
  4. Ratings from prior weeks only. A club is rated on official scores strictly before the graded week. Early-season rows rest on very little history and are marked.
  5. Two snapshots, not a path. Open and close hide the movement between them. A line that moved on injury news looks identical to one that drifted on volume.
  6. Depth for four clubs. Requirement 6 is satisfied for CHI, DEN, PHI and TB, and for no other club. The 28 uncaptured clubs are reported as uncaptured, not as healthy.
  7. No prop prices. Player-prop markets were reached but publish targets without two-way prices, so no prop strategy can be priced or graded.
  8. Paper only. Nothing was wagered, no reward is paid, and the ROI is a computation on a relayed number.

How to reproduce every figure

python3 nfl-market-desk/build.py            # write the artifacts
python3 nfl-market-desk/build.py --check    # reproduce and compare, byte for byte
python3 nfl-market-desk/site_build.py       # regenerate this site
python3 -m unittest discover -s nfl-market-desk/tests
python3 scripts/check_site.py               # links, sources, blank cells

The site reads only committed artifacts. No page computes a number at read time, so a page and its data cannot silently disagree: the build fails instead.

What would change the conclusion

  • Roughly 783 settled bets, with prices captured the same way, would let the signal be estimated rather than described.
  • A direct operator price source would turn every relayed number into a checked one and move gate MD-G01 from completeness to confirmation.
  • A positive, adequately powered closing-line value in the gate's own units would be the first forward-looking evidence of an edge. It has not been observed.
  • A depth chart for the remaining 28 clubs would let requirement 6 be claimed league-wide instead of for four clubs.

None of these is a modelling improvement. The model is not the binding constraint: the data is, and the desk says so.
