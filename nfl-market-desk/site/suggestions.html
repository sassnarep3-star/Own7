How to use this page

Each suggestion states a recommendation, the effort, why it is worth doing and the risk of doing it. The Decision column is deliberately empty: it is for you to choose. Nothing on this desk is implemented because it was suggested here; the suggestions that are already implemented are marked as such in their own wording.

The two that matter most

SG1 · Backfill weeks 1 to 8 prices. Every rate on this site is a description of 15 games. The capture method is proven; the constraint is call count, not uncertainty. This is the only item that changes what any number here means.

SG2 · Re-express the CLV gate in points of line. The gate currently requires basis points of implied probability while the data is in points of line, which makes it unsatisfiable by any capture. A gate that cannot be satisfied is decorative. Note the guard: the new threshold must come from the distribution of line moves, not from our own record, or it becomes tuning.
