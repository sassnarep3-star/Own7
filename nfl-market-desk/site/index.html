Market desk

Own lines, real prices, and a validation chain that can say no. Where the numbers fail, the failure is the finding.

The one-paragraph answer

This desk originated its own line for all 15 Week 9 games, stored 180 opening and closing price observations, and graded every bet against 136 official final scores. Its own model loses money, and the desk can show exactly why.

The side model went 2-13 at the open for -74.6% at flat stake. That is not bad luck. The model expects 9.26 wins on its own probabilities and got 2, which is p = 0.000167 against its own forecast. It is also less accurate than the price it is trying to beat: margin error 13.552 points RMSE against the market’s 10.007. A forecast with more error than the price, that then loses to the price, is behaving exactly as it should.

No strategy is live, no reward is paid, and the sample needed to settle the question is printed on this site rather than left implied.

The order of work is a design decision

The brief asked for the side first, then the total, then the moneyline. That order is enforced in the code rather than described in a document, because the order controls what each board is allowed to assume and how much independent evidence one forecast can claim.

Originated first

1 · The side

From the margin distribution only. The one board whose fair price needs no assumption about scoring level.

Originated second

2 · The total

From the same score pair, fitted and reported separately. Never rescaled to flatter the side.

Originated third

3 · The moneyline

Last, and labelled a restatement of the side in price space, so one forecast is never counted as three signals.

Price store

180 observations, every row naming its provider, its source and its retrieval date. All relayed, and labelled so.

Ordered origination

Model line, market line and the difference as three separate columns on every game, side then total then moneyline.

Leaderboard

Signal and staking in separate columns. Promotion reads the signal only, and the code refuses a staking field.

Correlation

Official-sourced numbers only, every value carrying its n, with Holm adjustment across the register.

Replacement depth

Which string dresses, not merely that someone is out. Four clubs have a captured chart; the other 28 say so.

Validation & demotion

Ten gates, each able to fail, plus five numeric demotion triggers that return a live strategy to paper.

What this desk will not do

  • It will not present a relayed price as an operator-confirmed one. No sportsbook was read directly.
  • It will not fill a missing price with a notional number. Where a price is absent, the figure is blank.
  • It will not promote a strategy for making money. Promotion reads the signal tier only.
  • It will not rate a week using that week’s own result. Ratings are rebuilt per week, on prior weeks only.
  • It will not claim requirement 6 league-wide. Depth was captured for four clubs and the other 28 are marked.
