Price store

Opening and closing numbers for every market originated, with the relay chain stated rather than buried.

What is in the store, and what it is not

180 observations across 15 games and 3 markets, all from a single provider: DraftKings (provider id 100).

Every row is a relay. The prices are DraftKings' numbers as ESPN republishes them. No sportsbook API or page was read directly, because direct HTTPS from the sandbox returns no response and the reads went through a page reader. The relay label is not decorative: a relay can lag the operator, re-round a price, or omit a market the operator was offering.

Nothing is averaged across providers. A single-provider series is the only thing from which a movement can be read without ambiguity, and two relays are still relays.

Basis: EC6-ESPN-ODDS

The schema, proven on one payload

Each odds payload carries, for the same competition and the same provider:

open.total / close.total                     the total line at each snapshot
open.over / close.over, open.under / close.under   the two-way prices on the total
open.{home,away}TeamOdds.pointSpread          that side's own spread, signed for that side
open.{home,away}TeamOdds.moneyLine            that side's moneyline

Two traps in the same payload, both of which would corrupt a naive capture:

  • The top-level details field names only the favourite, who may be the home or the away club. Reading teams from it binds the wrong side whenever the favourite is the visitor.
  • moneylineWinner and spreadWinner are present on settled games and are still false. They cannot grade anything. Grading is done against the official results file instead.
