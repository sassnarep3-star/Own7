The questions (10)
QuestionStatusThe answerDepends on
Q1Can this desk originate its own side, total and moneyline before the market moves, and show the difference?answered for week 9Yes, mechanically: three ordered boards on 15 games, each publishing model, market and difference. The answer is not 'yes, profitably'. See Q3.EC6-REPO
Q2Is the originated line better than the market line?answered noNo. On the same 15 games the model's margin error is 13.55 points RMSE against the market's 10.01, and its total error is 14.54 against 14.06. A model that is less accurate than the price cannot be said to have found an edge.EC6-REPO
Q3The side model's record is 2-13. Is that bad luck?answered noNo. The model's own probabilities expected 9.26 wins on its 15 bets and it produced 2, which is p = 0.000167 against its own forecast: it was overconfident, not unlucky. It is also less accurate than the market (13.55 points RMSE against 10.01). A model that is worse than the price and then loses to the price is behaving consistently.EC6-REPO
Q4Do the numbers come from the operator or through a relay?answered relayedRelayed. Prices are DraftKings' numbers as published by ESPN, read on 2026-11-14. No sportsbook API or page was read directly. Every price row is labelled relayed and the label is not decorative: a relay can lag, re-round, or omit markets the operator offered.EC6-REPO
Q5Can a strategy be promoted live on a good staking run?answered no and enforcedNo. The promotion function rejects a strategy outright if a staking field is present, so a profitable paper record cannot promote anything. Promotion needs 200 settled bets, a signal p-value at or below 0.05, a Holm-adjusted q at or below 0.10, verified prices and positive closing-line value. The separation also protects the other direction: the side board here has a p-value that clears 0.05 and a staking ROI of -74.6%, and neither figure is allowed to decide anything on its own.EC6-REPO
Q6Is there a live demotion gate?armed not yet exercisedThe gate is defined with five numeric triggers and is executable. Nothing is live, so no row has been demoted yet. That means the gate is implemented but unproven in anger, and it is reported as such rather than as 'operational'.EC6-REPO
Q7How deep is the replacement when a starter is out?answered for four clubsFor CHI, DEN, PHI and TB the chart was read in full, so the string that dresses is known. Of 25 players carrying an official game status league-wide, 5 have no healthy body printed behind them, 12 cause the club to reach one string down, and 8 cannot be placed because the player is not on the captured chart or carries no printed rank.EC6-REPO
Q8Can player-prop combos be priced from the captured data?answered noNo. The prop endpoint returns markets and their open and current targets, but no over or under price, and every row is duplicated. A combo cannot be priced without a price. This is recorded as a blocked capability rather than a strategy awaiting data.EC6-REPO
Q9Are the correlations in the register real?answered within limitsTwo of five pairs have enough observations to rank. The official-score correlations use n=136 finals (margin vs total r=-0.16, not significant after adjustment). The rest use n=15 and are shown with that n and marked unrankable, because at n=15 the interval on r spans most of its range.EC6-REPO
Q10What is the single largest gap between what is held and what the strategy needs?answeredSample, and it is no longer the only gap. Detecting a 55% rate against a coin at 80% power needs 783 settled bets and the capture holds 15. But the more immediate finding is that the model is worse than the market and significantly underperformed its own probabilities at n=15, so more data would most likely confirm the model is broken rather than reveal an edge. Data and model both need work, and the desk reports which is which.EC6-REPO

M7A CLV figure in the units the promotion gate requiresThe gate is written in basis points of implied probability; this capture measures CLV in points of line. M8A pre-registered sign convention, checked before any result was readThis desk shipped an inverted spread sign that turned a 2-13 model into a 13-2 record, and only caught it by hand-checking one game. The bug was invisible in every summary statistic, because a sign error produces a plausible story in whichever direction it points.trust in any result produced before the convention was fixedAlready closed for the sign: the conversion now lives in one tested function and two regression tests pin both the side and the total conventions. The general lesson is not closed: a convention that can invert a result should be asserted before the first result is computed, not after a human notices.EC6-REPO

Decisions taken while doing the work

Decisions and their consequences
DecisionWhat was decidedWhyConsequence
D1Refuse to stake at the closeStaking at the close makes closing-line value identically zero and assumes we could trade on information we publish late. Bets are taken at the open.Every ROI is an open-price ROI and is labelled as such.EC6-REPO
D2Report CLV in points, not basis pointsLines moved up to fourteen points on this capture; a probability-only measure reports near-zero for a move worth a dozen points of handicap.The promotion gate, written in basis points, is reported as unsatisfiable rather than quietly converted.EC6-REPO
D3Rate from official scores per week, never from a standings snapshotA standings page is a snapshot of now, so using it to rate an earlier week silently includes that week's own result.Ratings are rebuilt per graded week and the window is printed on every row.EC6-REPO
D4Make the promotion function raise on a staking fieldA rule in a document can be forgotten; a raise cannot. This is how 'a lucky run cannot mask a bad signal' becomes true rather than asserted.The leaderboard passes only signal fields and the guard is tested.EC6-REPO
D5Label every price relayed and never claim operator confirmationNo sportsbook was read directly. A relay may lag or re-round.Gate MD-G01 passes on provenance completeness and carries an explicit relay caveat rather than a clean bill.EC6-REPO
D6Treat the home side's printed spread as a LINE, not as a margin`market_line` is the home club's own spread, so a home favourite prints -9.5 and the market's implied home margin is +9.5. The first build compared the model's expected margin directly against the printed line, which inverted every side pick and produced a 13-2 record from a losing model.The conversion lives in one tested function, `market_home_margin`, and the total board has its own separate conversion because a total is not signed like a spread. Both conventions are pinned by regression tests.EC6-REPO
