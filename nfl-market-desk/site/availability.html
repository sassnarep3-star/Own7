Replacement depth

Which string dresses, measured per slot, with the four-club coverage stated rather than implied.

The distinction that matters

"Starter out" is not a price. Which string dresses is closer to one.

Two clubs can each lose a receiver and be in completely different shape: the club whose third receiver now plays is deeper than the club whose fifth does, having already lost the fourth. So the unit of record here is the slot, and the measure is how many strings the club had to reach down.

Coverage, stated up front: Depth charts were read for 4 of 32 clubs (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB). The other 28 clubs have no captured chart, so requirement 6 is satisfied for those four clubs only. No claim is made about the rest.

How each player is classified

Only a player with an official game status of Out or Questionable is treated as unavailable. Practice status alone is never treated as unavailability, because a full practice does not mean a player dresses and a limited practice does not mean he does not.

  • No healthy body behind — the club prints no available replacement in the slot. Structurally worse than a deep replacement, because there is no floor left at that position.
  • Reached down chart — an available body exists but at a deeper rank. Two or three strings is a different problem from one.
  • Like for like — the printed replacement holds the same string.
  • Rank not printed / not on chart — the chart does not place the player. The string is left blank rather than inferred from the row's position on the page.
