Failing a gate is not a finding about the market; it is a statement that the evidence needed to judge the strategy does not exist yet.

Why a control model is required

A strategy is only interesting if it beats a model that knows nothing except the league average and home field. ST-03 is that model. Publishing a score without it would make an ordinary prediction look like skill.

The eight strategies form one declared family. That is why the multiple-testing gate is procedural rather than optional: testing eight variants and reporting the best one is the classic way to manufacture a winner.
