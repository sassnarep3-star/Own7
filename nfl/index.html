What is verified today

Standings. All 32 club rows were read from the official standings page. Three independent arithmetic checks pass on every row: win percentage, net points, and the league-wide identity that total points for equal total points against (6,246 = 6,246).

Injuries. Practice participation for the Week 10 slate was read twice - once from the injury page and once from the official Wednesday roundup - and the two agree on every player named in both. Seven players are already ruled out.

Depth. Three club depth charts were read in full. One of them prints an update date; the other two do not.

What is missing

Prices. No verified market price exists, so no edge, return or closing-line value can be computed. The league odds path returned 404 and no official league price publication was located.

Sunday designations. The Game Status Report for a Sunday game is due Friday at 4 p.m. New York time. It does not exist yet, so any number published today would be built on Wednesday news.

Depth coverage. 25 of the 28 clubs in the slate have no depth evidence in this repository.
