Tracker design.

Official injury status is recorded verbatim. Replacement players (2nd/3rd/4th string) and quantified deficiencies are blank — the injury page does not state them, and rosters were not transcribed. No deficiency is asserted without evidence.

How deficiencies would be measured (once data exist)

  1. Map each Out/Doubtful starter to the official depth chart (2nd/3rd/4th string).
  2. Compare team offense/defense efficiency with vs without the starter (game logs required).
  3. Quantify with uncertainty; abstain when the replacement is unknown.
