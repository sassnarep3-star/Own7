Skill versus luck

Three things separate skill from luck here, and none of them can be shortcut:

  • A baseline. The control model, not the market, is the first hurdle.
  • A pre-registered test. The family of eight is declared before any result exists, and Holm adjustment is applied to the whole family.
  • An interval, not a point. A bootstrap interval on both the score and the return, with the sample size published next to it.

With 14 upcoming games there is nothing to test yet. Any claim of skill today would be a claim about a coin.
