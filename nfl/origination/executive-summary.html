Key findings

Depth, not injury, is the missing variable

Five first-string players in this week's slate have no replacement listed on their club depth chart at all - including San Francisco's left corner and nickel back, and Cleveland's WILL linebacker. For those players, 'next man up' cannot be evaluated at any price until someone is actually listed.

The injury picture is incomplete by rule

Wednesday practice participation is verified for 208 rows. Game designations for Sunday exist for none of them yet, because the policy deadline is Friday. Any Sunday price built today is stale by construction.

Every strategy is blocked for the same reason

Six of ten gates fail on the absence of a price. The protocol itself is implemented and specified; the input does not exist.

Luck is bounded, not removed

Skill will be measured as a Brier skill score against a home-field control model, with a bootstrap interval and Holm adjustment across eight declared strategies. None of that has been run, because nothing has been settled.
