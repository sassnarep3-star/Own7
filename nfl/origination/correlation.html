What would make a leg correlation publishable

One row per settled game containing the final margin, the final total and the result of each leg. From that, a joint rate and a correlation with an interval. A minimum of 100 joint observations is proposed before any value is shown.

Nothing in this repository currently produces that dataset, which is why the cells above are empty.
