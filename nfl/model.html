Model & lines

Where the numbers come from: official points for and against, turned into spreads and totals with an explicit range.

Every number on this page is derived from official standings rows, not from a market. The spread and total are published as a range across the parameter grid, because the parameters are design choices.See the flagged items

A value of 4 pseudo-games means a club with 8 games played is weighted two-thirds on its own rate and one-third on the league mean.

Formula

league_avg = mean(PF/G) over 32 clubs = 23.0104 off_adj(A) = (PF/G_A * G_A + league_avg * k) / (G_A + k) def_adj(A) = (PA/G_A * G_A + league_avg * k) / (G_A + k) expected_home = off_adj(home) * def_adj(away) / league_avg expected_away = off_adj(away) * def_adj(home) / league_avg model_margin_home = expected_home - expected_away + hfa model_total = expected_home + expected_away P(home win) = 1 / (1 + exp(-model_margin_home / sigma))

The model is deliberately simple and fully auditable. It is a line-origination baseline, not a claim of edge.
