ORIGINATION

How a number here is produced

Team strength from scoring

pyth_pct = PF^2.37 / (PF^2.37 + PA^2.37)

The exponent 2.37 is the published NFL convention, not a value fitted here. It converts a club's points for and against into an expected win rate, which is then compared with its actual record to produce a residual.

Source: a tertiary summary of the method, used for the method only. No NFL observation is taken from it.

PYTH-NFL ↗PFR-GLOSSARY ↗

One score pair per game

home = (home_off + away_def + league)/3 + hfa
away = (away_off + home_def + league)/3

The spread is the negated home margin and the total is their sum. The home adjustment is a project assumption of 2.0 points applied to every game, including the Munich fixture, and that is flagged rather than hidden.
