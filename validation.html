Validation gates

All nine gates are proposed project policies. No candidate has been evaluated or passed.

GatePurposePass rule (project design)Current statusOfficial / primary source
G01ProvenanceStore official URL, retrieval UTC, season, game/player IDs, raw response hash, schema and corrections. Reject duplicate or incomplete records.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G02As-of integrityFeatures and availability known strictly before prediction cutoff; freeze predictions before odds comparison. Keep final-game statistics out of inputs.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G03Locked testPre-register features, all attempted variants and disjoint train/tune/test date windows; no tuning after seeing test. Group related games/legs by date.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G04CalibrationImprove held-out log loss over intercept and de-vigged market; report Brier and reliability by probability band. No aggregate score substitutes for calibration.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G05Uncertainty and selectionProject rule: game-date block bootstrap, 95% interval for net ROI lower bound above zero; multiplicity-adjusted tests at 5% across all trials. Fix block length and minimum detectable effect before tests; reject underpowered entries.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G06Execution realismTimestamp actual offered prices and limits; include vig, rejected orders, slippage, void/push rules. Compare like-for-like closing lines as diagnostics, not proof of skill.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G07DependenceEstimate paired residual and binary-leg correlations with sample sizes and intervals; validate joint probability calibration. Reject combos without enough aligned observations.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G08RobustnessRequire positive direction in separate time blocks and predeclared forward-paper window; stress lineups, seasons and worse prices. Duration and power must be frozen before evaluation.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗
G09GovernanceReproducible code and seed, immutable entry IDs, disqualification for leakage, legal/operator availability checked, no real stakes or payouts without approved funding and limits.Blocked / not evaluatedSource ↗

Thresholds above are design choices, not externally verified guarantees. A gate is NOT passed by having a source link. Minimum sample size is deliberately not guessed: it needs an effect-size and dependence-aware power calculation before the first evaluation.
