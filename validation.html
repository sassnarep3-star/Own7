Luck is not removable

Separate development, calibration, validation and a sealed common final test in time. Keep all legs of a game together. Use pregame publication/capture timestamps, log every attempted model and rejected offer, and check dependence across dates and seasons.

A minimum count is merely a screen. Power depends on the expected effect, variance, stake policy and dependence. Positive CLV, a low p-value or a favorable short-run return alone does not establish transferable skill. A new frozen forward test is required after exploration.
