DEPENDENCE & UNCERTAINTY

Correlation is an estimate, not a guarantee

Use paired, pregame-model residuals from the same games to estimate Pearson correlation for continuous outcomes, or empirical joint hit rates for binary legs. Report the pair definition, sample size, date range, and game-clustered bootstrap interval. Fit on training data only; validate joint probabilities on later games. If variance is zero or the sample is insufficient, leave the value blank. Never assume independence just because a correlation estimate is unavailable.

↗
Luck cannot be removed.
Out-of-sample testing, clustered uncertainty, and accounting for the number of trials reduce the risk of mistaking noise for skill; they cannot guarantee future profit.
