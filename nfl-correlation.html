Why parlays need their own measurement

P(A and B) = p_A p_B + rho * sqrt(p_A(1-p_A) p_B(1-p_B))

This identity holds for two binary legs with matching marginals. Multiplying two prices silently sets rho to zero. For three or more legs even a full pairwise matrix is insufficient, which is why no combination product is priced on this site.
