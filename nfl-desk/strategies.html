Origination and signals

Eight declared strategies, all blocked, and the arithmetic behind them

Origination means writing a hypothesis and its kill condition before looking at a result. Eight were written first. Then the data was checked to see whether any of them could be tested at all. None could, for reasons stated per row.

Why every market field is empty

A strategy's line, price, return and closing-line value are the numbers that would make it actionable. No price source exists here, so all four are blank on all eight rows. This is not caution for its own sake: a fair-odds figure that cannot be taken is more misleading than an empty cell.

Strategy rows in full

S01

Pythagorean expectation residual · Single bets

Hypothesis
Clubs whose win total runs ahead of their scoring profile (Pythagorean expectation from points for and against) are more likely to regress than clubs whose record matches their scoring profile. The candidate is a spread or moneyline position against the over-performing club.
Inputs held
Points for, points against, wins, losses, ties from the official league standings table.
Test required
Walk-forward: compute the residual from games 1..k, then evaluate the next block. Requires a per-week historical snapshot that this repository does not hold.
What would kill it
If scoring-profile residual has no relationship to future point margin, the premise is empty.
Risk
Regression-to-the-mean language is often used to justify a bet that the market has already priced. Without a price, the residual cannot be converted into an edge.

S02

Scoring environment total · Single bets

Hypothesis
A club's own points for and points against per game, combined for both clubs in a fixture, gives a baseline expectation for the game total that can be compared with a posted total.
Inputs held
PF and PA per club from the official standings table; the Week 10 fixture list.
Test required
Compare the baseline total with realised totals across many weeks, not one week.
What would kill it
If the naive scoring average predicts totals as well as any adjusted model, the added complexity earns nothing.
Risk
The baseline ignores opponent strength, pace, weather and venue. Its error is unknown until it is scored.

S03

Availability shock · Single bets

Hypothesis
Clubs entering a fixture with more first-string players carrying a game status (Out, then Questionable) will under-perform their season baseline in that game, and their opponents will over-perform.
Inputs held
Week 10 official injury report; the three transcribed club depth charts; the fixture list.
Test required
Needs several weeks of injury reports archived with their report timestamps, joined to results.
What would kill it
If the market moves on the same report before the price is available, the information is already in the number.
Risk
A listed player is not necessarily a starter, and a depth chart is a coaching estimate rather than a lineup. Both facts are visible in this repository's data and are not smoothed over.

S04

Replacement-chain erosion · Player props

Hypothesis
When a club's published first-string player at a position is unavailable, the next listed player inherits opportunity. The prop candidate is usage (attempts, targets, carries) for the replacement, not a raw yardage projection.
Inputs held
Depth charts for the three clubs with transcribed charts; the official injury report.
Test required
Needs snap counts or per-game usage for replacements, which this repository does not hold.
What would kill it
If the replacement's usage is priced identically to the starter's, the chain carries no information.
Risk
Depth charts are unofficial and change without notice; a club site can list a player who does not play that role in the game plan.

S05

Completion-percentage persistence · Player props

Hypothesis
Club passing efficiency measured on a stable denominator (completions over attempts, and yards over attempts, computed from the same integers) is more persistent week to week than raw passing yards, and therefore more useful in a prop baseline.
Inputs held
Attempts, completions and the displayed completion percentage from the official team passing table.
Test required
Needs week-by-week passing splits, which the site exposes only as a season-to-date table in this capture.
What would kill it
If week-to-week autocorrelation is near zero, a season rate is not a projection.
Risk
Attempt volume and game script drive raw yards far more than efficiency. A persistence finding about rates would not transfer to yardage props.

S06

Cross-game combination screen · Parlays

Hypothesis
Legs drawn from different games are closer to independent than same-game legs, so a cross-game combination's fair price is closer to the product of its marginals. The candidate prices cross-game combinations only.
Inputs held
No leg-level price or result archive exists here.
Test required
Requires a leg-level archive with prices taken at a known time.
What would kill it
If even cross-game legs show measurable dependence through shared conditions (weather, referee crew, week-level effects), the product rule fails.
Risk
Assuming independence because games look unrelated is exactly the assumption gate G05 exists to stop.

S07

Same-game dependence penalty · Parlays

Hypothesis
Same-game legs are positively dependent when they share a side of the ball. A combination that quotes them as independent is over-priced relative to their true joint probability, which is a reason to avoid the combination rather than to bet it.
Inputs held
No same-game leg archive exists here.
Test required
Requires paired leg outcomes on the same game, which this repository does not hold.
What would kill it
If measured same-game leg correlation is negligible, the penalty disappears and the hypothesis is empty.
Risk
This is the one area where an honest research project can produce a useful conclusion that is simply 'do not buy this product'.

S08

Team-total and receiver combination · Prop combinations

Hypothesis
A receiver's yardage prop and the club's team total share a common driver (the passing game). The candidate measures that dependence before quoting any joint probability.
Inputs held
Club passing totals from the official team table; no per-player game logs are held.
Test required
Requires per-player game logs and team totals on the same games.
What would kill it
If the two legs show no measurable shared variation beyond the club's own scoring level, no combination premium exists.
Risk
Club-level correlation is not a player-leg correlation. Re-using it as one would be the exact error gate G05 names.

Measured dependence, club level only

These are correlations across 32 club-seasons from the official standings and team passing tables. Pearson is the linear coefficient; Spearman is the same computation on ranks, which is less sensitive to a single extreme club. The p-value comes from twenty thousand deterministic reshuffles of one column, so it describes how unusual this pattern would be if the two quantities were unrelated. A small p-value here does not mean profitable, and it does not mean a bet-leg relationship exists.
