Decision desk · read date 12 November 2026

Originate lines only after the evidence can carry them

An NFL research desk built to answer one question honestly: what would have to be true before a line, a signal or a strategy could be called qualified? This page states the answer, the evidence behind it, and what is still missing.

No line, price or edge is published here, and none has been earned.

This desk holds one week of official injury data, one season-to-date standings snapshot and three club depth charts. It holds no price history, no per-player game logs and no forward test. Every economic field is therefore blank by design. What follows is the decision record: what is known, what is missing, and exactly what would have to happen before any strategy could be called qualified.
