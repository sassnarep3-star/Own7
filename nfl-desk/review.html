DF-03

Official team passing table has unlabeled columns and an unreproducible rate

What is missing
The captured table renders 15 numeric columns with no header row. Attempts, completions, completion percentage and yards were identified because they reproduce each other arithmetically. The displayed per-attempt average could NOT be reproduced from those integers on four of 32 rows (KC 7.745 shown as 7.8; JAX 8.546 as 8.6; SEA 7.253 as 7.2; PIT 6.452 as 6.4), so that column is excluded and yards per attempt is published only as a derived value. Touchdowns, interceptions, longest and five further columns are unidentified and are not published.

Disposition

How to close a flag

  • DF-01 closes when a licensed, timestamped price archive is contracted and its operator settlement rules are attached to each market.
  • DF-02 closes when injury reports are captured as dated snapshots rather than read as a live page.
  • DF-03 and DF-04 close when a source table with readable column labels is captured, at which point the excluded columns can be transcribed with a stated definition.
  • DF-05 closes when head-to-head and division records are held, which requires a schedule and results archive.
  • DF-06, DF-07 and DF-09 close club by club as depth-chart URLs are verified and archived with dates.
  • DF-14 closes only with written permission, or with a decision that the work stays a bounded, non-commercial, attributed snapshot.
