Availability and depth

Who is listed, who is out, and who replaces whom

This page separates four things that are usually collapsed into one injury number: a player being listed, a player missing practice, a player carrying a game status, and a player being ruled out. It then shows replacement chains only for the clubs whose own depth chart is held.

Two limits decide how this page can be read

The official report is a single undated page state, so it cannot tell you what was knowable before a specific kickoff (flag DF-02). Club depth charts are unofficial coaching estimates and are held for three of thirty-two clubs (flag DF-06). Neither limit is worked around by estimation.

Reading the table

  • WAS: 23 chart entries, 4 of them on the report, 0 ruled out. Two listed players were ruled out — a centre and a tight end — and both have a charted replacement, so the chain is explicit rather than guessed.

Coverage

Clubs absent from the report, and why that is not a health claim

  • BAL: the page rendered 'No Injuries Reported' instead of a player table. This means the club did not list anyone on that page at that moment.
  • LAC: the page rendered 'No Injuries Reported' instead of a player table. This means the club did not list anyone on that page at that moment.

  • The remaining unlisted clubs are accounted for one by one: twenty-six clubs did list players, two rendered the no-injuries note, and four were on bye. That arithmetic closes, which is why no club is silently missing.
