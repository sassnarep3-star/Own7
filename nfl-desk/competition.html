Competition and validation

A leaderboard nobody can win by getting lucky

The competition is specified so that the mechanism, not the promise, keeps luck out. Nothing is ranked today because no entry has cleared a single gate. An empty board is the honest state of a competition whose admission rules are actually applied.

No reward of monetary value exists

Four tiers are defined and all four are non-monetary. No prize is funded, authorised or promised, and the monetary-value field is blank on every tier. A funded cash prize would require both a funding decision and a legal review by jurisdiction, neither of which has happened (flag DF-13).

A gate is never marked passed because the evidence is likely. Reproducibility (G10) is the only half-met gate: the build is deterministic and hash-checked, while the publishing rights half is unresolved. The pass rule and the exact reason each gate fails today are written out in full below.

Gates in full

G01

Provenance and source rights

Is every input traceable to a primary source that permits this use?

Pass rule
Each input row links to an official source URL that was actually read, with a read date and a stated scope. A second, independent check reproduces the row. If the source's terms do not permit the intended use, the gate fails regardless of data quality.
Where it stands today
Partially met. Row-level official links exist and are re-checked by the build. NFL.com Terms §1.3 limits use to individual non-commercial informational purposes and prohibits systematic retrieval or compilation; no gambling or commercial permission is granted. Commercial or betting-price release therefore fails this gate today.

G02

Temporal integrity

Was every input available before the event it is used to predict?

Pass rule
Each feature carries the timestamp at which it became public. A prediction may use only features published before its own kickoff. Any feature whose publication time is unknown fails the gate rather than being treated as pregame.
Where it stands today
Not met. Injury-report page states carry no per-report timestamp in this snapshot, only the Week 10 designation. Standings and passing totals are through each club's last completed game, so they cannot be replayed for a single week without a historical archive.

G03

Coverage and statistical power

Is there enough independent data to distinguish signal from noise?

Pass rule
The candidate must report n, the number of independent units, and a confidence interval for its claimed effect. An interval that cannot exclude the no-effect value on the pre-declared sample fails. Selection of the sample after seeing results fails.
Where it stands today
Not met. The largest verified cross-section here is 32 club-seasons, and the only per-event sample is a single week (14 games). 32 units can detect only large correlations; a single week cannot support a season claim.

G05

Joint dependence for combinations

For any parlay or prop combination, is the dependence measured rather than assumed independent?

Pass rule
A combination may only be priced from a joint probability that was measured on the same events, or from a stated copula with its assumption disclosed. Multiplying marginal probabilities is a failing answer for same-game or same-player legs unless the measured dependence is shown to be negligible.
Where it stands today
Not met. No same-game leg-level data is available. The only measured dependence in this repository is club-season correlation, which is a different unit and cannot be reused as a leg correlation.

G06

Executable economics

Did the candidate survive the actual price and the actual settlement rule?

Pass rule
Every claimed return must be reproducible from a timestamped, executable price that a retail bettor could have taken, net of vig, and settled under the rules of a named operator in a named jurisdiction. If no auditable price history exists, the claimed return stays blank.
Where it stands today
Not met, by design. No licensed price feed is connected and none is claimed. NFL.com publishes no odds, and the repository holds no operator price archive. Every price, hold, ROI and closing-line field is blank.

G07

Uncertainty and robustness

Does the result survive reasonable re-specification and resampling?

Pass rule
Report a resampling interval, the effect of dropping the largest contributor, and at least one plausible re-specification (a different but defensible window, weighting or control). A result that reverses under those is recorded as fragile, not qualified.
Where it stands today
Partially met for the descriptive correlations: permutation p-values are reported. Not met for any candidate strategy, because no candidate has an out-of-sample sample to resample.

Not met. The eight hypotheses in the strategy ledger were written before scoring, but no correction has been applied because no candidate has a testable sample yet.

G09

Forward shadow and risk

Did the candidate survive untouched forward observation, within a stated risk limit?

Pass rule
A minimum of one full forward period with the rules frozen and no edits to the candidate. Stake sizing, maximum drawdown and stop conditions must be declared in advance. Any mid-period change resets the clock.
Where it stands today
Not started. Zero forward periods have elapsed. Nothing here has been bet, simulated for money, or staked.

Scoring rules that remove luck

How an entry is scored

How an entry is scored. Blank cells are deliberate: no verified value exists.
IDRuleMechanismWhy it stops luck from winningSources
K1Skill score, not accuracyEntries are ranked by a proper scoring rule against the declared naive baseline (log score or Brier for probabilities, absolute error for totals). Hit rate alone is not scored, because it hides stake and price.A 55% hit rate at a bad price loses money; ranking by hit rate rewards luck and punishes honesty.REPO
K2Luck-adjusted intervalEvery entry is ranked with an interval that accounts for its number of independent events. Ranking uses the lower bound, not the point estimate.Ranking on point estimates means the entry with the most variance can win a small sample by chance.REPO
K3Declared trial countThe number of variants each entrant tried is declared before scoring and the significance level is corrected for it.Without this, the leaderboard rewards whoever searched hardest, not whoever found something real.REPO
K4Frozen forward periodAn entry becomes eligible only after one full forward period with its rules frozen and untouched.This is the only mechanism here that measures skill rather than fit.REPO
K5Reproduction by a second partyAn independent rebuild from the committed snapshots must reproduce the entry's numbers exactly before it is ranked.An unreproducible number cannot be checked for error, so it cannot be compared.REPO
K6Withdrawal for unrealised claimsAny claim of profit or edge without an executable, timestamped price is struck from the entry, not merely footnoted.A claim that cannot be settled is not a result.REPO

Policy

Anti-luck rules in force

  • No ranking from a single game, a single week, or any sample smaller than the pre-declared minimum.
  • No ranking of an entry whose price is unknown, because the return cannot be computed at all.
  • No 'best of N' display: every declared variant stays visible with its own result.
  • No mid-period rule change. A change resets the entry's forward clock to zero.
  • No reward is denominated in money unless a funded prize is separately documented; none is.
  • Ties are broken by the entry with the wider evidence base, not by the better point estimate.
