PRESENTATION RULE

Fair line, market line and difference

For spread and total, the comparison is model fair point line − market point line, shown separately for opening and closing. For moneyline, the model fair probability is compared with the raw probability implied by a captured American price; it includes vig and is not labelled an edge. If either input is absent, the difference is blank.

The Week 10 model values are derived from the checked standings and the project model. The official NFL schedule verifies the event identity, not a market quote or the model assumptions. See the source-linked line board and price-slot matrix.
