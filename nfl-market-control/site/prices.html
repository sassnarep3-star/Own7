DATA CONTRACT

Store contract

The machine-readable append-only store is data/quote_snapshots.jsonl. The 84-row matrix represents every originated game/market/selection; each row expects an opening and a closing observation. It is not evidence that an operator offered a line. Future records are rejected unless their market/selection is valid, source ID is registered, HTTPS host matches that source, timestamp includes a timezone, rights are documented as authorized, source status is verified, and an evidence SHA-256 is present.

Opening and closing are stored per operator, not averaged across books. A duplicated slot or a closing timestamp earlier than opening is rejected. The exact timestamp cutoff, settlement/void rules, feed provider and use-rights still require a documented decision before any quote can be ingested.
