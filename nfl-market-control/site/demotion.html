PERFORMANCE STOP

Statistical performance trigger

Conservative non-positive lower bound

After 100 settled bets in the locked evaluation cohort, demote from live to paper when the pre-registered one-sided 95% lower confidence bound for net ROI is at or below 0%.

After at least 100 settled bets in the locked evaluation cohort, demote when the pre-registered one-sided 95% lower confidence bound for net ROI is at or below 0. The bound must account for the registered sampling/dependence design and the full tested strategy family.

The 100-bet floor is an explicit project-policy choice carried from the existing results gate, not an NFL rule or universal power claim. The gate remains inactive until the ROI method accounts for dependence, the full trial family, authorized prices and complete settlement.

NO AUTOMATIC RE-PROMOTION

Re-entry rule

  1. Demotion always returns the strategy to paper; it never deletes losing or missing rows.
  2. Fix the triggering integrity, rights, settlement, risk or performance issue and freeze a new model/data version.
  3. Repeat every relevant provenance, temporal, sample, calibration, multiplicity, price, rights and risk gate on a new prospective shadow record.
  4. Require a human review decision in the decision register before any future live designation. There is no automatic promotion in this repository.
