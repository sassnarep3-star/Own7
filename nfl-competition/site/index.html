The one-paragraph answer

This lab builds the eight things the brief asked for and then shows what each one is actually worth. The results feed is real: 137 official final scores that re-derive into every club's record and match the standings page on all 192 club fields. The price store is real too, and it is empty, because nfl.com returns a 404 at /odds/ and prints no price anywhere this project read. Everything that needs a price - the difference column, the staking table, return on investment, closing-line value, every reward - is therefore blank, and blank is published rather than estimated. What does work is the part that needs no price: the board is originated side first, then total, then moneyline; replacement depth is measured from each club's own printed chart; leg dependence is measured from real joint outcomes; and not one strategy survives multiplicity control, so nothing is promoted and nothing is paid.

The eight requirements

Price-snapshot store

The store, its schema, its capture instants and its contract. Zero rows, and the verified reason why.

Verified results feed

137 official scores, each with its page, reconciled against the standings.

Side, then total, then moneyline

Three stages in a fixed order, each reading only the stage before it.

Fair line, market line, difference

Three separate columns. The third is blank whenever the second is.

Signal and staking apart

Two tables that never touch. Staking can demote an entry and never promote one.

Depth of the replacement

Not just that a starter is out, but how far down the chart the club must reach.

Correlations with n

Only from officially sourced numbers, and every row states its sample size.

Demotion gate

Ten triggers that send a live strategy back to paper, with today's reading on each.
