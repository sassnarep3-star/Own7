What is true, with a source

  • 137 official final scores are captured. Re-derived into club records they match the NFL.com standings on 192 of 192 fields, with 6291 points scored each way. A backtest can be settled.
  • 13 Week 10 games are originated, in three stages. One Week 10 game had already finished when the schedule was read, so it is excluded from the board and carried in the results feed instead.
  • No price exists. nfl.com returns HTTP 404 at /odds/, and no page this project read prints a spread, a total or a price. The store is built, its contract is tested, and it holds zero rows.
  • No strategy is skill based yet. The best directional record is 74-46 (61.7%), which does not survive multiplicity control at an adjusted p of 0.10 (q = 0.115).
  • 8 starters are ruled out on a printed chart; 7 are replaced by a second-string player and one slot has no printed replacement at all.

What is not true, and is not claimed

  • No return on investment, stake, drawdown or closing-line value is published anywhere. Every one of those cells is blank.
  • No reward is paid, and no prize pool exists. The schedule is published; the winner is not, because there is no winner.
  • No player-prop combination is graded. The division exists and both of its entries are blocked on data, not on effort.
  • No point value is attached to an injury or to a replacement. A printed rank is an ordering, not a measurement of ability.

The three things that block everything

  1. No executable price (SG-01). Until one is captured, the staking half of the leaderboard and every reward stay blank.
  2. Source rights (SG-12). NFL.com terms section 1.3 restrict systematic retrieval without written consent. Decide this before building anything that captures prices.
  3. Sample size (SG-05). Detecting a true 60% rate at 80% power needs 153 settled selections; 55% needs 617. The largest sample here is 120.

What to read next

20 questions are catalogued, of which 4 are open and 1 is blocked on rights. 15 irregularities are flagged, of which 6 are open and 2 were defects in this lab's own code, fixed before publication. Start with the suggestions: they are ranked, and the decision record is waiting for a choice.
