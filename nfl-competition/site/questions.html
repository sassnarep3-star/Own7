How to read this

A decision cannot reasonably be made until these are answered. Each question states what is held, with its official source, what is missing, and the action that would close it.

Statuses: answered - an official source answers it; answered-no - an official source answers it and the answer is no; partial - some is measured, the rest is not published; open - not answerable from anything retrieved; blocked - answerable in principle, but the NFL.com terms stand in the way.
