The order is structural, not a comment

Stage 1 produces one coherent score pair and the side from it. Stage 2 is handed that pair and may not model points again. Stage 3 is handed the stage-1 margin and the measured margin spread and may not re-model the score. A stage missing an input it was not given raises instead of guessing.
