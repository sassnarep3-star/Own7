Fair vs market

The model's number, the market's number and the difference, as three separate columns that never substitute for each other.

What each column means

  • fair_line: The model's number, from the three-stage origination.
  • market_line: No consulted official source publishes a price or a point spread for this market (see the nfl.com/odds/ negative result, CP-NFL-ODDS-ABSENT). Blank, not zero.
  • difference: fair_line minus market_line. Blank whenever the market line is blank; never printed as zero.
  • fair_price: The model's fair, zero-hold price. Not an offered price.
  • market_price: No consulted official source publishes a price or a point spread for this market (see the nfl.com/odds/ negative result, CP-NFL-ODDS-ABSENT). Blank, not zero.
  • price_difference: Blank whenever the market price is blank.

Why the difference is blank and stays blank

  • NFL.com returns a 404 at /odds/ and prints no spread, total or price on any page this project read, including the Week 10 game page.
  • No operator or sportsbook was consulted: the NFL.com terms restrict use of the material to individual non-commercial informational purposes, and the project has no written consent to retrieve prices systematically.
  • A difference is the whole point of the column, so it is left empty rather than filled with an estimate that would look like an edge.

A difference is the whole point of the column. Printing a zero there would look like a finding, and printing the fair line twice would look like a comparison. Neither is honest, so the cell is empty.

How the over price is derived

The one fair price on the board that is not definitional is the over. It comes from the walk-forward residuals rather than from a guess.
