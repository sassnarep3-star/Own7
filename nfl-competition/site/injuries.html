Limits

  • A printed rank is an ordering, not a measurement of how good the replacement is. No point value, probability or line adjustment is produced from one, because no snap count, target share or performance log was retrieved to justify it.
  • The league prints a game status on a minority of rows, so this table is a floor on each club's depletion, not a complete count.
  • Houston's chart renders as an image on its club site, so Houston has no printed ranks and its depth is blank rather than guessed.
  • Chicago's chart is dated 10/31/2026, before the ledger it is joined to; Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa print no date; Philadelphia's chart is self-labelled unofficial.
