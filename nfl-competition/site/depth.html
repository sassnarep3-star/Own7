Replacement depth

How far down its own printed chart a club must reach - a second-string replacement is a different problem from a fourth-string one.

8starters ruled out
7replaced by a 2nd string
0replaced by a 3rd string
1slots with no printed replacement

Definitions

  • replacement_depth: The printed rank, on the club's own depth chart, of the first name in the slot who is not ruled out. 2 means a second-string player steps in; 3 means a third-string player; and so on.
  • reach: replacement_depth - 1. How many printed names the club must go past.
  • slot_exhausted: Every printed name in the slot is ruled out, so no replacement is printed. Published blank and flagged, never filled with a guess.
  • no_backup_printed: The club prints one name in the slot and no one behind him.

The ceiling on how much of this can be known

  • 326 injury rows were captured.
  • Only 25 of them carry an official game status.
  • The other 301 carry a practice status and no game status, so no absence is stated and no replacement is implied.
  • 31 clubs have a captured chart; HOU does not.
  • 1815 printed slots in total.

A replacement is only implied where the league prints a game status. For the other rows the report states a practice status and no game status, so no replacement is implied and no depth is computed.

What a rank is not

  • No point value, probability or line adjustment is produced from a depth. A printed rank is an ordering, not a measurement of how good the replacement is, and no snap count or performance log was retrieved to turn it into one.
  • Snap share, target share and usage are not published by the official injury report, so the 'next man up' impact on a projection stays blank.
  • Houston's chart renders as an image on its club site, so Houston has no printed ranks and its depth is blank.
