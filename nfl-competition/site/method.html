Method & limits

How every number on this site is produced, and the four things this lab will not do to get one.

What this lab is

An NFL research desk that originates its own lines, grades them walk-forward against official final scores, and publishes what it knows next to what it does not. It produces no price, no stake, no return, no wager and no reward, and it attaches no point value to an injury. Nothing here is a betting service or betting advice.

How a forecast is made

  1. Cumulative scoring rates are built from official final scores in weeks strictly before the game being forecast.
  2. Stage 1 produces one score pair and the side from it.
  3. Stage 2 takes that pair and sums it for the total.
  4. Stage 3 takes the stage-1 margin and the measured margin spread and produces a win probability and a fair, zero-hold price.

The home allowance and the margin spread are measured from prior games at each step, so no week sees its own result.

How a strategy is graded

  • A forecast for week w uses only weeks less than w. Week 1 is never scored.
  • The permutation null shuffles forecasts across games, keeping the forecasts and the results exactly as they are and destroying only the pairing, so a low p-value means the forecast was matched to the right game.
  • Adjusted p-values use Benjamini-Hochberg at 0.10 across every strategy tested in the same run.
  • A forecast is never graded against itself, except as a published control.

What is never done

  • No value is inferred, interpolated or filled in. A cell with no official source behind it is empty.
  • No market line is estimated from a model line, and the difference column is never printed as zero.
  • No staking figure is produced without a price, and the staking table can never raise a signal rank.
  • No parameter is fitted on the evaluation window.
  • No dataset is released. NFL.com terms section 1.3 prohibit systematic retrieval to compile a collection without written consent, and no consent is evidenced.
