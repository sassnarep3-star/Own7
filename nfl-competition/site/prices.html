Why it is empty

  • nfl.com returns HTTP 404 at /odds/; the rendered response contains no odds table, no price, no operator and no market list (CP-NFL-ODDS-ABSENT).
  • The official Week 10 game page read on 2026-11-14 prints a score, a by-quarter table, game info and a standings widget, and prints no spread, total or price.
  • No operator or sportsbook was consulted. NFL.com terms section 1.3 permit individual non-commercial informational use and prohibit systematic retrieval to compile a collection without written consent; no such consent is evidenced.

Capture instants, fixed in advance

  • opening: First capture at or after kickoff minus 48 hours.
  • closing: Last capture at or before kickoff minus 90 minutes.
  • usable_for_clv: A market is usable only when it holds one opening row and one closing row for the same operator.
  • why_fixed_in_advance: A capture instant chosen after the result is known lets a favourable number be selected retrospectively. Fixing the instants in advance removes that degree of freedom.
  • late_move: A move after the closing instant is recorded as a third row with capture_point = POST_CLOSE and is never used for a closing-line value.

What unblocks it

  • A written licence or terms of use that permits retrieving and retaining opening and closing prices from a named operator, or
  • an official league publication that prints opening and closing numbers, which would then be registered in sources.py like any other source.
