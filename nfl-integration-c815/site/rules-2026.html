2026 kickoff / onside-kick regime

Requirement DR-68 was Open because the 2026 kickoff and declared onside-kick rules had not been transcribed from the official rulebook. They are transcribed here, and each value is extracted by pattern from the capture — if the wording ever changes, the build fails instead of carrying a stale number forward.

What changed for 2026, in the rulebook's own words

6-1-3 alignment in the setup zone; 6-1-5 the dead-ball spot after a touchback when the kickoff is from the 50; 6-1-6 permits the kicking team to declare an onside kick at any time during the game; 19-2 official consultation on disqualifications.
