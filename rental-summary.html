SF / RENTAL ANALYSIS

Rental summary

The important details of the SF 94122 rent-increase analysis in one place.

Research JSONResearch only
Bottom line: the lawful, normal increase is the published annual allowable increase — 1.6% for 3/1/2026-2/28/2027 — applied uniformly to both units.

For Unit 1 that is +$63.92 (to $4,058.92); for Unit 2 +$50.40 (to $3,200.40). Above-cap amounts require a Rent Board petition, and every increase needs a current rent increase license plus proper written notice.
