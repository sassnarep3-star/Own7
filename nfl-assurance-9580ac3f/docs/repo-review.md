## Boundary

Full line coverage means **inventory/hash/syntax coverage**, not independent scientific, legal or factual verification of every assertion inside the inherited repository. The baseline contains NBA research, operator/market evidence and rental sections alongside coordination history. Those older claims are preserved and are **not** promoted to NFL evidence.
