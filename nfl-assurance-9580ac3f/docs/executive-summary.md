# NFL assurance executive summary

Checked: 2026-11-12 UTC. This is an independent, bounded assurance supplement to the canonical NFL research site (`nfl/`) and the nested line-origination lab (`nfl/origination/`). It does not replace either.

## Bottom line

The handoff is sufficient to audit evidence and block unsupported claims. It is **not** sufficient to originate verified NFL betting lines, estimate bet-leg correlations, qualify a strategy, or award a prize.

- **Injuries:** 276 official Week 10 rows across 26 teams. Game status: 5 Out, 1 Questionable, 270 blank. A blank game-status cell is not a clean bill of health or a final active list. [NFL injury report](https://www.nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/REG10), [policy announcement](https://www.nfl.com/news/competition-committee-approves-revisions-to-injury-report-0ap3000000688693).
- **Depth:** 289 club-published positional slots across ten clubs (113 offense, 116 defense, 60 special teams). Shared printed cells and interior blank columns are preserved. Two slots have a first-string Out; three have a later-listed Out. Actual replacement and performance deficit are null. Club charts can be explicitly unofficial even on official club domains. [Patriots chart](https://www.patriots.com/team/depth-chart), [Steelers chart](https://www.steelers.com/team/depth-chart/).
- **Repository:** 150 files / 14,244 text lines / 2,004,428 bytes inventoried and hashed at frozen baseline `a989d67`; syntax errors `[]`. This is mechanical line coverage, not independent factual verification of every old NBA/rental assertion. [Baseline repository](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/Own7/tree/a989d67d9155b43ee2b93c39cbfdde54d8dd0b09).
- **Rights:** NFL.com terms §1.3 restrict systematic retrieval/commercial use; §12 prohibits using Services to wager on NFL games. No separate license is evidenced. Informational audit only; NFL-derived betting-price release, automated bulk collection and wagering remain disabled. [NFL terms](https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms/).
- **Markets:** No authorized timestamped offers, fitted scoring panel or aligned settled prop thresholds are present. Actual lines, correlations, ROI and CLV remain blank. Synthetic engine tests are not NFL observations. [Project repository](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/Own7/tree/a989d67d9155b43ee2b93c39cbfdde54d8dd0b09).
- **Validation:** Twelve fail-closed gates are implemented. With no verified artifacts supplied, zero gates pass and no strategy qualifies. The leaderboard is empty; the competition design awards non-transferable merit points only, with no cash pool. [Holm method](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.multitest.multipletests.html), [calibration method](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html).

## Luck and skill

Luck cannot be eliminated. Proper scores, chronological splits, multiplicity correction and date-block uncertainty reduce avoidable evaluation error; they do not remove random outcomes or guarantee skill. Minimum sample floors (500 selections / 200 games / 60 dates; 60-day / 200-game shadow) are project policy, not official skill standards.
