Dependence is estimated from aligned outcomes, never assumed away

No verified NFL leg correlation is published

Pearson/Spearman, binary hit-event phi, empirical joint rates, Fréchet bounds and paired/date-block bootstrap primitives are implemented and tested on synthetic inputs. Actual NFL values require aligned settled legs, exact thresholds and operator settlement rules. Pairwise correlations do not identify three-plus-leg joint probabilities.
