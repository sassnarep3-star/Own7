Playing time is the one official measurement that says who actually played, rather than who was listed.

The NFL Game Book publishes, for every player in every game, the number of snaps he took on offense, on defense and on special teams, together with the league's own percentage of that unit's total. This desk transcribes that table, re-derives every percentage from the published unit total, and keeps the ones that disagree.

One week is one sample.

Reliability here is computed over the Game Books captured so far. Intervals are wide on purpose and are printed with the number of games actually observed, so a rate is never read as if it rested on a full season.

Every snap count and every share on this desk is transcribed from the official NFL Game Book PDF for that fixture, whose "Playtime Percentage" table the league itself labels Percent of playtime per player on offense, defense and special teams (Unofficial). Where the official artefact does not publish a value the cell is blank and the row appears on the flagged page — nothing is inferred, imputed or back-solved.
