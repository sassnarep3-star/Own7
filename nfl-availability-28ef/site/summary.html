Executive summary

The findings that matter and the gaps that still block a decision.

Read on 2026-11-13. Everything below is either printed by an official source or left blank.

Five findings

  1. The official injury report lists 326 players across 28 clubs for Week 10. Only 25 rows carry a game status (14 Out, 11 Questionable). The other 301 are blank on the page itself.
  2. 10 printed starters carry a game status: 7 ruled out, 3 questionable. Four of the seven are defenders, two are offensive players and one is a long snapper.
  3. In six of seven cases the printed chart names a second string as the next man in. In one case - the Colts long snapper - the chart prints no further name at all.
  4. One chain is already two deep: New England's printed starting running back is questionable and the printed second string is out, which leaves the third string as the next available name.
  5. Twenty-seven of the 28 clubs playing this week have a printed chart. Houston's page returned no chart, so its 16 injury rows have no rank and no replacement - they are published blank.

Four gaps that block a decision

  1. Game status is published for 5 clubs only. Until a club or the league publishes Friday statuses for the rest, no depletion count can be league-wide.
  2. Club charts are 'unofficial'. Several publishers say so on the page. They are printed opinions, not confirmed game-day personnel.
  3. No market price exists in the record. So no depletion finding can be turned into an edge, a stake or a reward yet.
  4. No results and no inactives. The only list that decides who plays is the official inactives list issued shortly before kickoff; it has not been read.
