What this desk answers

When the league lists a player as Out, Doubtful or Questionable, what does the club's own printed chart say about who is behind him? That is the whole question. The answer is published per player, with the printed chain, the next name in line and the source of both.

It deliberately does not produce a point value, a win probability, a price or a bet. No performance deficit is estimated, because no official source publishes one and inventing one would be fabrication.

How to read a blank

An empty cell marked with a pale background is a value an official source did not publish. It is not a zero, not a guess and not a pending calculation. On the league injury report, 301 of 326 rows carry no game status at all - that blank is the finding, not an omission.
