Suggestions for review

Choose a path before anything is implemented.

Proposals, ranked by the decision they unlock. Nothing on this page has been implemented, and nothing will be until a path is chosen below. Choosing a path here records a local preference only - it does not authorise work, spend money or change the published data.

How the review workflow works

  1. Each proposal states its evidence, what it unlocks, its cost, its risk and an acceptance test.
  2. A reviewer chooses Adopt, Defer or Reject for each row, with a reason.
  3. The choices are saved in this browser only and can be exported as JSON, so a decision can be carried to the person who implements it.
  4. Nothing changes on the site until an adopted proposal has been implemented and re-verified against official sources.

Default state is 'no choice recorded'. The recommendations above are proposals, not decisions.
