Leaderboard charter · project design

Separate singles, multi-game parlays, individual props and same-game combos. Freeze each submission, date cutoff, code hash and prediction log; all nine gates must pass. Rank qualifying entries by held-out log-loss improvement against a common benchmark within the same market cohort; tie-break by net ROI lower confidence bound, then lower drawdown. Do not compare raw log loss across unrelated event types.

Publish total bets, effective game clusters, exposure, ROI interval, closing-line diagnostics, calibration and rejected trades. Keep all attempted variants in the trial registry, including failures. The proposed reward is a non-cash research badge after an independent forward-paper replication. Cash amount and funding remain blank; nobody is promised a payout. No winner can be selected from the current evidence.
