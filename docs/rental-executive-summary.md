## Bottom line
The reasonable and normal increase for these rent-controlled units is the **San Francisco Rent Board's published annual allowable increase: 1.6% for increases with an effective date between March 1, 2026 and February 28, 2027** (60% of the Bay Area CPI-U change), imposed **once per 12 months**, calculated on **base rent only** and **never rounded up**.
- **Unit 1:** $3,995.00 × .016 = **+$63.92 → $4,058.92**
- **Unit 2:** $3,150.00 × .016 = **+$50.40 → $3,200.40**

The state cap (AB 1482: min(5% + regional CPI, 10%) = **8.8%** for the San Francisco Area for increases starting 8/1/2026–7/31/2027) is higher, but the **lower local cap controls** for units under the SF Rent Ordinance. No federal rent cap was located; federal law (Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. §3604(b)) prohibits discrimination in rental terms.

## Key rules (all officially sourced — see site Source register)
- **Coverage:** most units built on or before June 13, 1979 are rent controlled → the 1940 building is covered. ([sf.gov rental laws](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-san-francisco-rental-laws/))
- **Rate:** 1.6% for 3/1/2026–2/28/2027; prior periods 1.4% (2025–26) and 1.7% (2024–25). ([sf.gov news](https://www.sf.gov/news--annual-rent-increase-3126-22827-announced), [sf.gov rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit), [sf.gov rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco))
- **License:** a current rent increase license (via the Rent Board Housing Inventory, annual deadline March 1) must be on file on the effective date, or the notice is unenforceable. ([sf.gov Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory))
- **Notice:** 30-day written notice; 90 days if the increase alone or combined in the same year exceeds 10%; +5 days if mailed; notice must state dollar amount, percentage, effective date, and any banked portion; use the rate in effect on the effective date. ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco), [Civ. Code §827](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=827.))
- **Banking:** a skipped increase is banked only after 24+ full months without any increase; no compounding; does not carry to a new tenant. ([sf.gov banked increases](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases))
- **Above-cap increases:** only via Rent Board landlord petitions / passthroughs. ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco))
- **Roommates:** no additional rent solely for an added occupant. ([sf.gov](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco))
- **State:** [Civ. Code §1947.12](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1947.12.) and [CA AG rent-cap chart](https://oag.ca.gov/rentcaps); [AB 1482 bill text](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billCompareClient.xhtml?bill_id=201920200AB1482); CPI source [BLS SF-Oakland-Hayward](https://blsmon1.bls.gov/regions/west/news-release/consumerpriceindex_sanfrancisco.htm).
- **Federal:** [Fair Housing Act, 42 U.S.C. ch. 45](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title42/html/USCODE-2023-title42-chap45.htm); [HUD FHEO](https://www.hud.gov/contactus/fairhousing).

## Recommendations
1. Apply the **same lawful percentage (1.6%) to both units** on their anniversary dates with identical procedure — uniform treatment avoids any fair-housing / discrimination inquiry.
2. Confirm the **rent increase license** is current before serving any notice.
3. Serve **30-day written notices** (mailed: 35 days) stating dollar amount, percentage, effective date.
4. Do **not** charge extra for Unit 1's changed occupants.
5. Leave the **2027–28 rate blank** until the Rent Board announces it; verify **Unit 2's banked-increase history** from owner records before adding any banked amount.
6. Have a **SF tenant-rights attorney** review the actual notice — tenants can obtain free counsel (Prop F, [Eviction Defense Collaborative](https://evictiondefense.org/faq-items/what-is-tenant-right-to-counsel-prop-f/)) and free Rent Board counseling (415-252-4600).
