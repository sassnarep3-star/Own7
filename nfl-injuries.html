What the official categories mean

Practice participation is Did Not Participate, Limited (less than 100% of normal reps) or Full (100% of normal reps). Game status is Out, Doubtful or Questionable. The "Probable" category was eliminated, so a player certain to play is not listed on the game status report at all.

What is missing for the owner requirement

The request asks for second-, third- and fourth-string exposure on both sides of the ball. Meeting it needs a published depth order per position plus recent snap share, so that a listed absence can be converted into a change in expected output.

Neither was retrieved. The depth-chart address on NFL.com returned a 404 to the reader, and no snap-count page was obtained. Rather than estimate, the fields stay blank and the gap is published.
