What verification here does not cover

These checks establish that this desk faithfully reproduces the official artefacts it read. They do not establish that the official artefact is itself correct, and they do not establish anything about prices, edges or profitability. No market price exists on this desk.

Every value on this desk is transcribed from an official NFL Game Book PDF linked from the official Game Centre, or from the official NFL injury report. Where an official artefact does not publish a value the cell is blank and the row is listed on the flagged page — nothing is inferred.
