INTERPRETATION

Reading the numbers honestly

Fourteen players carried an Out designation. Ten appear on their club's official Not Active list; four do not. Those four are named on the flagged page. That is a real irregularity in the official record, not a modelling choice, and it is exactly why a fixed "Out means zero" assumption is unsafe.

Eleven players carried Questionable. Three were inactive. With n this small the interval spans a quarter of the probability range, so the desk publishes the estimate and explicitly withholds any recommendation to price off it.
