WHAT THIS CHANGES

Why the Game Book matters

Three requirements stop being estimates.

The repository previously inferred starters from depth charts and inferred inactives from the weekly injury report. Both are proxies. The Game Book is the primary record: it names the eleven players each club actually started on offense and defense, and it lists every player who was Not Active. That converts three standing requests from inference into transcription:

  • Who was expected to start, who actually did - the availability graph, now sourced on both sides.
  • Was a "Questionable" player really inactive? - measured here, not assumed from legacy percentages.
  • Venue, roof and weather - transcribed per fixture instead of left blank.
BOUNDARIES

What this desk does not claim

The honest limit is stated before the numbers.

The Game Book does not publish snap counts, depth rank, or the reason a player was inactive. So this desk does not rank backups, does not compute a depth penalty, and does not claim a replacement's quality. It states the replacement's name where the official lineup proves it, and leaves depth rank to the clubs' own charts - which this repository has captured for only four of thirty-two clubs.
