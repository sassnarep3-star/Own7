Sign convention, stated so the numbers cannot be misread

A line is written from the favoured side as a negative number. So a home line of -7 means the home club is favoured by a touchdown, and the expected home margin is +7. That makes the EXPECTED correlation between the signed home line and the home margin NEGATIVE, and between the closing total and the actual total POSITIVE. A coefficient with the opposite sign to this convention would mean the number is pointing the wrong way, not that the forecast is strong.

Scope

Games in scope: 136; forecast rows: 120. Computed only from registered official numbers. n is printed on every value. Anything without an official source is blank and named, never assumed to be zero.

Two of these values are ours and four are the market's. Putting them side by side is the point: it is the only honest way to say how much sharper the market is without claiming we can match it.
