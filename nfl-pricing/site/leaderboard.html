Hit rate counts beating the published closing number, not the model's own line. Units are an accounting convention at a stated assumed price with no observed hold: they are not a profit claim and they never rank an entry.

What the split actually caught

Best staking record: S07 at 5.909 units and a 0.5763 hit rate. Best signal record: S07 at 0.5763. Same entry: True.

Here the two records coincide, so the split rescued nothing. It did the other job: a single units column would have hidden that several variants sit at or above break-even on hit rate while their units column is negative, and it would have hidden the reverse too.

If the best staking record and the best signal record are different entries, ranking on units would have promoted the wrong strategy. That is the case the two-column split is designed to expose.
