OWN7 · Price & market desk

Suggestions

Ranked proposals to review before anything is implemented. Each states its evidence in the numbers, what it unlocks, what it costs, what could go wrong, and a runnable acceptance test. The decision columns are blank on purpose: nothing here acts on itself.

How to use this page

  1. Check the evidence against the number quoted on the page it comes from. If they do not match, treat the proposal as unproven and say so.
  2. Read the risk before the benefit. The most common failure is implementing a proposal on the same games that motivated it.
  3. Record adopt, defer or reject against the ID. Nothing is implemented by being listed, and this build does not choose on your behalf.
  4. Hold the implementation to the acceptance test. If the test cannot be run, the proposal is not ready, however good the idea sounds.
