Why the stored number is called a close

The publisher prints exactly one number per game and does not label it an open or a close. It is recorded here as the closing snapshot because it is the last published pre-kickoff number on that page. No opening number is estimated, interpolated or back-filled from line movement.

Kind of number held: published_reference_price_not_executable_offer. A published reference price is not an executable offer: no book, limit, hold or account is implied by this record.

Provenance: the page itself credits historical line data to Sports Odds History, so even a correctly transcribed number is a second-hand record of someone else's market.

Paper research only. No wager, stake, bankroll or monetary reward is produced anywhere on this desk. A market line here is a published reference price and not an executable offer, and no edge is claimed.
