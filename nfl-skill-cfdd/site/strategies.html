Why every strategy is blocked, including the good-sounding ones

Ten strategies are defined across single bets, two-leg and three-leg parlays and a player-prop combo. Every one of them is BLOCKED, and the reason is always the same two gates, plus one of the data gates:

  • V8, blocked on rights. There is no price. NFL.com publishes no odds and the terms restrict systematic retrieval, so no market value exists to compare a model against. This is not laziness and not a missing download - it is a permission question, and suggestion P02 is the way to close it.
  • V4, failing on sample size. 153 settled bets minimum at a 60% rate. There are zero.
  • V9, failing on forward record. A prospective record cannot be created retrospectively, and any attempt to would be the exact hindsight this project is built to avoid.

The parlays and the prop combo carry two extra failures, V3 and V6, because their leg dependence has never been measured. They are listed anyway, at the bottom of the leaderboard, so you can see they were not quietly dropped.
