The distinction that matters

You asked for correlation values for parlays and player-prop combos. What is measurable here is club-level correlation: how one club-season quantity moves with another across 32 clubs. That is a genuinely different quantity from leg correlation, which is how two outcomes on the same ticket move together.

Club-level r cannot be substituted for leg r. A negative club-level correlation between points scored and points allowed says nothing about whether two legs of a parlay are independent. Publishing one as the other would be the single most tempting error in this whole project, so the two are kept apart by name and the leg version stays blank.

Read the one significant result carefully

Pythagorean expected wins against actual wins gives r = 0.867 and a Holm-adjusted p of 0. That is not a finding. Both sides are computed from the same PF/PA pair, so a high correlation is arithmetic, not evidence. It is published as a validity check on the method and it carries that caveat in its own row. Flag CF09.

Meanwhile the two genuinely independent comparisons - points for against points against, and offensive passing efficiency against defensive passing efficiency - both straddle zero after multiplicity control. That is the honest state of the evidence.
