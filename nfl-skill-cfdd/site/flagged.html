How to read this page

These are published rather than fixed, because most of them are true facts about the sources, not defects in this desk. Two of them are blocking:

  • CF07, only 4 of 28 clubs publish a game status. This is the single biggest limit on the availability work. Most clubs show a zero depletion index because nothing is provable, not because nobody is hurt.
  • CF10, no price exists anywhere. This is a rights question, not a data gap, and every economic claim is blank because of it.

The "explained" flags are still worth reading - CF08 and CF09 are the two places where a reader could easily draw the wrong conclusion from a real number on this site.
