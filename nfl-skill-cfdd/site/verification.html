What was withheld, and why

the three unnamed 3-part records, because the rendered column order does not identify which is HOME, ROAD, DIV or CONF the two unnamed decimal columns for the same reason.

The standings page legend names HOME, ROAD, DIV and CONF, but the rendered table prints three 3-part records and two decimals in an order that does not identify which is which. Two of them could be guessed with high confidence; guessing is what this project does not do. They are absent from the table rather than wrong in it. This blocks strategy S9 (divisional underdog) and any home/road split, and it is question Q03.
