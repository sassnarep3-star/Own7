The reward policy, and why it pays nothing yet

You asked for a leaderboard competition that rewards top-performing strategies. The format is published here and the board is live. It is empty of scores because a reward paid on noise teaches the project to prefer noise.

The reward rule, stated before any result exists so it cannot be fitted afterwards:

  1. V4 must pass. At least 153 settled independent bets per strategy.
  2. V5 must pass. The strategy must beat a stated naive baseline out of sample, not merely beat break-even.
  3. V9 must pass. A dated forward shadow record, committed before kickoff, with a verifiable hash chain.
  4. V8 must resolve. No reward may be attached to a strategy whose price basis does not exist.

Until all four hold, the board ranks evidence debt and pays nothing. 0 rewards paid

What a populated board would need to do differently

When the gates clear, ranking changes from "fewest missing gates" to a proper score. The intended score is a proper scoring rule on the shadow record - a Brier score for probability forecasts - reported with its interval, not a raw win rate. A raw win rate on a small sample is exactly the noise this page exists to refuse.
