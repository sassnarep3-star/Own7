The one-paragraph answer

This desk re-verified 32 club standings rows against the official publisher on 2026-11-14 and explained the only two that moved. It captured 4 printed depth charts that were missing (CHI, DEN, PHI, TB) and transcribed 231 slots, which raises the injury-to-chart match from the earlier desk to 265 of 326. It then ran the test that answers your actual question - can luck be removed - and got a hard answer: the Pythagorean residuals give a chi-square of 19.03 on 32 degrees of freedom, p = 0.966. That is inside the null, so there is no club-level skill this data can detect, and a leaderboard built on eight or nine games would be ranking noise. Zero strategies qualify and zero rewards are paid. Every parlay, prop combo and correlation stage is blank because no price and no settled bet exists anywhere in the repository, and the rights question is unsettled.
