Why these four clubs were missing

Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay had no captured chart in any earlier snapshot. The reason was never written down, and it turns out to be simple: they were the four Week 10 bye clubs. A bye club has no Week 10 injury row, so the missing charts never surfaced as unmatched injury rows. Nothing was hiding; the gap was just silent.

All four are now captured and joined, so they are ready for Week 11 rather than rediscovered then.

Chart quality is not uniform, and the page says so

Chicago prints a chart date of 10/31/2026, which is earlier than the injury ledger it is joined against. Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay print no date at all. Philadelphia labels its own chart "UNOFFICIAL" and still lists two players its own news feed reported waived on 13 November. All four of these facts are flags CF03, CF04 and CF09, not footnotes.
