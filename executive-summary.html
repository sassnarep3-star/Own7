One paragraph, if you read nothing else.

The NFL section is built, sourced and reproducible, and it overturns nothing: the scoring information is real, the originated lines are coherent, and the market comparison is simply absent. Until a dated price, depth and snap data, and a leg-dependence estimate exist, no NFL strategy may be ranked or rewarded.

The decision that is ready

Publish this as a non-commercial research note with every blank visible.

  • Whether to treat the 32-club standings snapshot and the Week 10 injury listing as adequate scope for a research note (yes, with the stated limits).
  • Whether the originated Week 10 numbers may be shown as a model exercise (yes, labelled, with no market comparison).
  • Whether any strategy may be ranked or rewarded (no).

The decisions that are not ready

  • Whether the originated lines beat, match or lose to the market: no price was retrieved.
  • Which clubs are relying on second- or third-string players: no published depth chart and no snap counts were retrieved.
  • Whether the Pythagorean signal has forward predictive value: one partial season, in-snapshot scoring only.
