Executive findings

Official results make retrospective tests possible

The results desk records 149 final scores (weeks 1–10 of 2026; one Week 10 game was not final at the read) and reconciles 320 standings fields. This is a saved-results pipeline, not a multi-season or prospective test. Official NFL schedule/results ↗ · Results audit

Signal is not yet deployment evidence

One bounded retrospective spread board reports 77–43 (64.2%; permutation p=0.0021; BH q=0.022) over 120 scored games; its total board reports 60–60 (50%). These are analyses, not official NFL statistics, proof of future skill, or profit. Read the complete selection, holdout and uncertainty limits. Official outcome source ↗ · Skill and luck analysis

Market prices exist in separate research desks

A price desk contains 136 historical closing-line observations attributed to Pro-Football-Reference, not an official NFL source. The lifecycle desk leaves its market cells blank pending its own price-policy gate. Do not combine these as a canonical price feed or claim unified ROI/CLV. Publisher source information ↗ · Pricing desk · Lifecycle policy and ledger

Injury reports are not participation evidence

Dated reports and club depth transcriptions support a preliminary replacement analysis, but chart rank is not playing time. Chart freshness, shared/empty slots, roster changes, and Houston’s image chart remain flagged. Official inactives, Game Books and snap counts are needed before confirming who replaced whom. Official NFL injury report ↗ · Replacement-depth analysis · Availability flags

Correlation is narrowly measured

The results desk reports spread/total phi −0.068 (p=0.47, n=114) for its bounded same-game sample. This neither validates independence nor supports player-prop combo pricing. Parlays and combos stay blocked until direct joint probabilities and settlement rules are validated. Official outcome source ↗ · Correlation analysis

Leaderboard and demotion gates are research controls

There are no strategies qualified for live use or rewards. Signal and staking are separated; the documented demotion gate has not been exercised on a live entry. Results leaderboard · Signal vs staking · Demotion gate

Questions that block a reasonable decision

  1. Which price source is permitted? Missing: recorded rights decision, one market universe, opening/closing timestamp convention, vig and settlement/void rules. Clear with an authorized provider, frozen policy and field-level coverage validation. NFL Terms ↗
  2. Does the signal generalize? Missing: a pre-registered model, multiple chronological seasons, untouched final holdout and prospective shadow forecasts. Clear with version-frozen rules, benchmark-relative proper scores, uncertainty and multiple-testing control. Official result source ↗
  3. Who actually replaced the starter? Missing: point-in-time official inactives/Game Books, transaction-aware roster evidence and snap counts. Clear by joining expected starters to actual participation and playing-time evidence; do not infer from jersey number. Official injury report ↗
  4. Do injury labels add information? Missing: multi-season designation-to-play rates and uncertainty. Clear with chronological, identical-model ablations with and without verified availability; use surprise/change features rather than fixed penalties. Official NFL injury report ↗
  5. Can parlays or player-prop combos qualify? Missing: calibrated joint probabilities at the relevant thresholds and operator-specific push/void rules. Clear with prospective joint calibration; do not multiply marginal probabilities. Official NFL outcome source ↗
  6. What sends a live strategy back to paper? Gate rules exist but have no live operational history. Clear by shadow-testing alerts/rollback, freezing thresholds and publishing trigger counts before any candidate is considered live. Official NFL Terms ↗

Suggested sequence — review before implementation

  1. Resolve source rights and price policy; do not publish unified market residual, ROI, CLV or rewards before that gate.
  2. Pre-register one singles-only shadow baseline, benchmarks, sample windows and gates before the evaluation window.
  3. Build permissible point-in-time availability evidence with dates, hashes and conflicts; capture inactives and snap counts only from authorized sources.
  4. Run chronological injury/personnel and fixture-context ablations with uncertainty; keep totals visible beside competing estimators until walk-forward comparison.
  5. Keep parlays and prop combos blocked pending validated joint evidence and settlement rules.
