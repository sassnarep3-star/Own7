Player prop signal framework

Player inputs come from the verified top-15 scorers table (2025-26 regular season, per game) sourced from NBA.com. Prop lines (points/rebounds/assists over-under) are not published by NBA.com and are blank + flagged unverified on every row — capture from a sportsbook at bet time.

  • Points prop: player PPG vs sportsbook points line.
  • Rebounds prop: player RPG vs sportsbook rebounds line.
  • Assists prop: player APG vs sportsbook assists line.

Flagged — prop lines

Player prop market lines are blank. No inferred values are used; a prop combo strategy cannot pass Gate 1 until line inputs are captured from an operator and verified against that operator's published line.

Player prop combo construction

A same-game prop combo combines 2+ player props in one game. It is leaderboard-eligible only if it passes Gates 1–5, including the correlation gate (docs/correlation.html). Examples below are analytical rationale only; numeric correlation values are blank and unverified until computed from official play-by-play.

Positively correlated combos (skill rationale)

  • Playmaker points + teammate finisher points (same team): a high-usage creator's scoring is associated with teammates' scoring opportunities (assists → points). Rationale: shared game script and playmaking gravity.
  • Big-man points + rebounds (same player): scoring possessions and rebounding opportunities both scale with minutes and usage in the paint.
  • Pace-driven team total + player points (same game): higher pace raises expected possessions for both teams.

Negatively correlated combos (avoid stacking)

  • Two high-usage players on the same team: possession competition reduces the probability that both exceed their lines simultaneously.
  • Teammate assists vs same player's turnovers: opposing directional dependencies within one game script.

Flagged — correlation values

Numeric correlation coefficients (rho) for every combo are blank. Computing them requires official play-by-play game logs (stats.nba.com API), which is unreachable from this environment; nba.com HTML pages do not expose play-by-play data. Values are never inferred.
