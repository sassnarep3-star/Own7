Purpose

Own7 originates NBA betting lines from official statistics, runs signal strategies as a selectively skill-based leaderboard, and removes luck by requiring reproducible, source-verified inputs with validation gates. Bet classes: single bets (spread/total/moneyline), parlays, and player-prop combinations.

Data basis (official, NBA.com)

  • 2025-26 regular season team stats — all 30 teams, complete 82-game season (source CSV)
  • 2025-26 regular season player stats — top 15 scorers, per game (source CSV)
  • 2026-27 standings — in progress as of 2026-11-03 (5–8 games per team; small sample, context only) (source CSV)
