Parlay framework

A parlay combines 2+ single bets into one wager. It pays only if all legs win.

  • Payout (decimal odds): product of leg decimal odds.
  • Joint probability under independence: P(A & B) = P(A) × P(B).
  • Fair parlay odds from model: fair odds = 1 / joint probability.
  • Edge: model joint probability − implied joint probability of the sportsbook parlay price.

Correlation handling for parlays

  • Cross-game legs (different games): treated as independent unless a shared-factor rationale (e.g., both teams on back-to-backs) is documented and sourced.
  • Same-game legs: require correlation disclosure; positive correlation admitted only with a repeatable mechanism and observed co-occurrence from official play-by-play.
  • Independence assumption: always labeled explicitly when used; never silently assumed.

Documented formulas (reproducible)

  • Implied probability: p_implied = 1 / decimal_odds
  • Vig removal: normalize implied probabilities so they sum to 1 across outcomes
  • Model edge: edge = p_model − p_implied
  • Correlation: ρ = cov(A,B) / (sd(A) × sd(B))
  • Parlay payout = Π decimal odds of legs
