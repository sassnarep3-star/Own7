Single-bet signal framework

Signals are computed from official NBA team statistics only (source data). Each signal type below is a rule applied to the verified table; the market line itself (spread/total/moneyline odds) is not published by NBA.com and is left blank, flagged unverified, pending capture from a sportsbook at bet time.

  • Spread signal: team point differential (+/−) vs opponent point differential; compared against the sportsbook spread.
  • Total signal: team PTS per game + opponent PTS per game vs the sportsbook total.
  • Moneyline signal: team win percentage vs implied probability of the sportsbook moneyline.

Flagged — market lines

Spread, total, and moneyline values are blank on every row. NBA.com publishes official statistics, not betting odds. Lines must be obtained from the operator at bet time; until captured, no single-bet strategy passes Gate 1 (official-source verification of the line input).
