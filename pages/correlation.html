Correlation definitions

  • Pearson correlation between legs A and B: ρ = cov(A,B) / (sd(A) × sd(B)), where ρ ∈ [-1, 1].
  • ρ > 0: legs move together (positive correlation). Joint probability under dependence exceeds P(A)×P(B).
  • ρ = 0: no linear correlation; independence approximation P(A & B) = P(A)×P(B).
  • ρ < 0: legs move in opposite directions (negative correlation); stacking them compounds risk.
