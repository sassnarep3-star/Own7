OFFICIAL DEFINITIONS QUOTED

NBA.com glossary, verbatim

  • OffRtg — "Measures a team's points scored per 100 possessions."
  • DEFRTG — "The number of points allowed per 100 possessions by a team."
  • NetRtg — "Measures a team's point differential per 100 possessions… Formula OFFRTG - DEFRTG."
  • PACE — "The number of possessions per 48 minutes for a team or player."
  • TS% — "A shooting percentage that factors in the value of three-point field goals and free throws… Formula Points / [2*(Field Goals Attempted+0.44*Free Throws Attempted)]."
  • Poss — "The number of possessions played by a player or team. Please note: an Offensive Rebound does not create another possession, it simply makes the existing possession longer."
  • PIE — "Player Impact Estimate… measures a player's overall statistical contribution against the total statistics in games they play in."

NOT USED, AND WHY

Third-party odds and data were deliberately excluded

Sportsbook aggregators and odds APIs were not used, because the project rule is official sources only and no official NBA source publishes prices. Secondary stat sites were also excluded so every value traces to the league itself. The cost of that discipline is visible on this site: no market comparison, no player-prop modelling, and a correlation sample of 18 games.
