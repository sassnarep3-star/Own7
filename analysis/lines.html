THE MATHS

How each number is produced

1. Shrink the rating

net_shrunk = μ + (gp / (gp + k)) × (net_raw − μ) μ = possession-weighted league mean NetRtg, k = 20 games

A team that is +12.8 after 8 games keeps 8/28 = 28.6% of that edge, because most of an 8-game rating is noise. This is the single most important defence against pricing a hot start as if it were a true rating.

2. Convert rating to points

gap_points = (net_shrunk_home − net_shrunk_away) × matchup_pace / 100 model_margin = gap_points + home_court_points (home_court = 2.5)

3. Turn the margin into a probability

model_margin ~ Normal(model_margin, 12.5) P(home wins) = Φ(model_margin / σ)

4. Turn the probability into a price

fair_decimal_odds = 1 / p

At p = 0.50 the fair price is 2.00. A book offering 1.91 is holding about 4.7% — which is why a 52.4% win rate, not 50%, is the real break-even at standard two-way prices.

PARLAY ARITHMETIC

What a two-leg parlay really costs

Two legs priced as independent multiply. If the legs are correlated, that multiplication is wrong, and the size of the error is computable:

P(A ∩ B) = Φ₂(z_A, z_B; φ) — bivariate normal CDF independence_gap = P(A ∩ B) − P(A)·P(B)

The engine implements Φ₂ directly and the self-tests pin it against the exact closed form P(X<0, Y<0) = 1/4 + arcsin(ρ)/2π. What the engine will not do is invent φ: it has to come from measured paired outcomes, and that measurement is on the correlation page.
