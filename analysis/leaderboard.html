THE KILLER NUMBER

With 200 bets at -110, a no-skill bettor's win rate still has a standard deviation of about 0.035. Among 100 identical no-skill entries, about 50 will show a profit. A leaderboard at 200 bets ranks noise.

This is why the ranking uses a lower confidence bound rather than ROI. Two strategies with the same ROI but different sample sizes are not equally good, and a bound makes the shorter record rank below the longer one automatically. The reward for a top-performing strategy therefore requires defining the evaluation window first — see the open owner decisions.
