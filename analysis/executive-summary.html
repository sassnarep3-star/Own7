THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION

Summary

READ THIS FIRST

A reproducible engine now originates NBA spread, total and win-probability lines from official NBA.com team ratings, with small-sample shrinkage so a hot start is not mistaken for a true rating. It measures real same-game dependence instead of assuming legs are independent, and it runs eight validation gates with three-state outcomes before anything reaches a leaderboard. On the current data the honest reading is this: the machinery is sound and independently testable, but the evidence base is too thin to rank any strategy. Eighteen games are not enough to pin a correlation, no official source publishes sportsbook prices, and player-level prop data could not be collected at all. The leaderboard therefore ranks nothing — deliberately.

DELIVERED

What exists now

  • Verified data layer — 30 teams and 18 games, every value traced to an NBA.com URL with a read timestamp.
  • Line origination — 14 model spread/total/win-probability lines for the 2026-11-04 slate, with assumptions named.
  • Correlation measurement — Pearson and phi with Fisher intervals, including a row deliberately flagged as a tautology.
  • Skill-vs-luck battery — exact binomial, probabilistic Sharpe ratio, deflated Sharpe ratio, minimum track-record length, block bootstrap.
  • Eight validation gates and a leaderboard that withholds ranks from unproven strategies.
  • 51 self-tests, including closed-form checks on the bivariate normal CDF and a byte-for-byte determinism test.

BOTTOM LINE

What to trust on this site, and what to doubt

Trust: the data (each cell has an NBA.com URL), the arithmetic (51 tests, closed-form anchored), and the gate logic (three-state, so "unknown" cannot become "pass").

Doubt: the originated lines. They are a state model over a partial season with three documented constants, and no market exists to check them against. They are a starting point for line origination, not a set of prices.

Reject: any reading of this site as evidence of a profitable system. Zero strategies are ranked because zero could be shown to be skill rather than luck, and that is the mathematically correct conclusion rather than a shortfall of effort.
