WHAT WOULD CLOSE THESE

The shortest honest path to a real edge

  1. Get a licensed, timestamped odds feed. Nothing on this site can become a bet evaluation until real prices with a capture time exist. This is the single blocking dependency.
  2. Get paired player game logs. Player-prop combos need per-player, per-game stat lines plus the exact prop lines and settlement rules for pushes and voids.
  3. Build a full-season game sample. A correlation needs roughly 174 paired games to reach ±0.15, and preferably two seasons to check stability.
  4. Pre-register the strategy, then leave it alone. Hash the thresholds before the first bet. The moment a threshold moves after results are seen, gate G1 fails.
  5. Define the reward and the evaluation window. Owner decision, not a modelling decision.
