## Note on the injury module wording

Every one of the twelve fixtures with no player rows prints the sentence
`Time<Club> have no active injuries at this time.` The leading `Time` is the accessible label of a
clock icon rendered inline by the publisher; it is transcribed here because it appears in the
text. The sentence is a placeholder for "this module is empty", **not** a statement that the club
is healthy — on the Colts at Texans page the same module prints player rows, and no club in the
league has yet filed a Week 11 practice report. It is treated as missing data throughout.
