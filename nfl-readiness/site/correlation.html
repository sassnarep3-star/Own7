R45 / R46

Parlay gate

JG-1

A same-game combination may be ranked only when a directly validated joint probability exists for that exact pair of thresholds AND the operator's void/push rule is registered. Multiplying two marginal probabilities is not a joint probability.

Combos ranked: 0. Marginal products used as a joint probability: 0.

The measured phi above is the right input for the first condition, but it is measured on the originated benchmark rather than on a market line, and no operator rule is registered, so the second condition fails and the gate stays shut.
