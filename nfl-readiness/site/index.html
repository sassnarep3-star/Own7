What the evidence says about the signal

The best of 21 singles entries is M04-SIDE: Brier skill +0.0571 on 57 holdout games, week-clustered lower bound +0.0154, multiplicity-adjusted q 0.060.

A single test against chance gives p=0.0166, which looks like a finding. Judged against the whole family of 21 trials it is p=0.348: the best of that many coin flips reaches this record 0.296 of the time.

A 57-game evaluation window cannot separate a 55% model from a 50% one: at 80% power a 55% model betting the breakeven rate needs 2,241 settled selections, which is 173 weeks at this season's 13 games a week, or 8.2 full 272-game seasons. At 57.5% the requirement falls to 585.

What the evidence says about availability

347 injury-report rows across 28 clubs, read on 2026-11-17. 171 carry a game designation.

64 of those players appear on one of the 6 club depth charts this desk read, and 44 have a printed name behind them on the chart. 283 do not appear on a chart at all and are marked as such rather than guessed.

Zero absences are confirmed, because no inactive list, Game Book or snap count is captured. That is why the personnel ablation has an empty arm.
