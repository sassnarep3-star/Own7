A 57-game evaluation window cannot separate a 55% model from a 50% one: at 80% power a 55% model betting the breakeven rate needs 2,241 settled selections, which is 173 weeks at this season's 13 games a week, or 8.2 full 272-game seasons. At 57.5% the requirement falls to 585.

R102

Why luck cannot be removed yet

Four structural reasons

  • One season of scored games, so no season-level holdout exists (PG-SEASONS).
  • No executable price, so a probability better than chance cannot become a return (PG-PRICE-SOURCE).
  • No pre-kickoff forecast has been recorded, so nothing here is prospective (PG-PROSPECTIVE).
  • The development and evaluation windows order the entries differently, which is what noise looks like.

Rejected alternative. Rank strategies by season-long return. Return is one draw from a distribution whose spread at n=57 dwarfs any plausible edge. The power table above is the arithmetic: a 55% model needs 2,846 settled selections to be seen at 80% power. Ranking on return would order the table by the noise term. Ranking on a cluster lower bound of a proper score at least orders it by evidence, and still refuses to qualify anything.
