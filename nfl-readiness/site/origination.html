Why the order is side, total, moneyline

The stages are run in this order because a side price comes from the margin distribution, a total from the same distribution's scoring level, and a moneyline from the margin distribution without a line. Running the total first would let a total assumption leak into the margin.

Each stage depends on the one before it, and the dependency is published so a reader can see that a total assumption is never allowed to reach the margin.
