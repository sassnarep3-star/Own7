What “skill based” means here

Luck cannot be removed. The proposed competition rewards repeatable out-of-sample probability quality and positive net returns with uncertainty controls, rather than streaks. A historical edge can fail in a new market regime. The evidence necessary to distinguish skill from luck is not yet present.
