Removal of luck

Is the best record skill, or chance?

The best record in the 30 graded variants is BOARD-SA (originated spread board), with a 82–51 record (61.7%). Luck is screened with a single-test p-value, a family-wise correction across the tests, and a permutation test. These are statistics on past games against a neutral league-mean line. They are not a price, an ROI or a closing line.
