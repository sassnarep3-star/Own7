How signal, economics and staking are kept apart
  • Signal gate: minimum forward sample, lower 95% skill bound over the frozen baseline, and familywise multiplicity control.
  • Economics gate: independently requires a positive lower 95% net-return bound after actual costs and a matching settlement contract.
  • Risk gate: checks per-game/day units and drawdown separately. Stake ROI is not used to rank or rescue the signal.
  • Any missing field is a failure for a live strategy; the default state is paper.
