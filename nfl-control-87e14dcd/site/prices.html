Definitions and future capture contract
  • Side: home spread points (negative means the home team is favored); total: game points; moneyline: home win probability.
  • Difference is defined only for a matching market/settlement contract. For moneyline, it is model home probability minus de-vigged market home probability from a complete paired quote.
  • Each market has an opening and a closing quote slot in the linked append-only store. Required fields include operator, capture time, exact source URL, authorization reference and settlement contract.
  • There are no values to fill from the checked NFL odds route, so no market line, price or difference is inferred.
