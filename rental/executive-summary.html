What is a reasonable and normal rental increase for these units in 94122 under local, state, and federal law?Local: the SF Rent Board annual allowable increase - 1.6% of base rent for 3/1/2026-2/28/2027 - once every 12 months, plus any banked increases; 1940 building is rent controlled. State: AB 1482's 5%+CPI/10% cap does not apply because stricter local rent control governs (1947.12(d)(3)); the state notice statute (Civ. Code 827) still applies. Federal: no rent cap; only the Fair Housing Act's ban on discriminatory terms applies.SFGOV-ANNUAL-26 ↗ SFGOV-RENTAL-LAWS ↗ CA-CC1947-12 ↗ CA-CC827 ↗ FHA-3604 ↗
How do big real-estate corporations handle increases vs individual owners in San Francisco?Legally the same Rent Ordinance rules apply to both for a rent-controlled building; the corporate distinction exists only in the state single-family/condo exemption. Day-to-day operational differences are business practice and are unverified here (see Flagged for Review).SFGOV-RENTAL-LAWS ↗ CA-CC1947-12 ↗
What is the correct way to inform tenants, and what are reasonable reasons?Serve a written notice (30 days for increases of 10% or less combined over 12 months; 90 days above 10%; +5 days if mailed) stating the dollar amount, percentage, effective date, and any banked portion, after confirming the rent increase license via the Housing Inventory. Reasonable reasons: the annual allowable increase, banked increases, and Rent Board-approved passthroughs. Charging more for an added roommate is not allowed.SFGOV-RENT-INC ↗ SFGOV-BANKED ↗ SFGOV-PETITIONS ↗ SFGOV-RENTBOARD ↗ CA-CC827 ↗
Can the increase pass review by SF tenant advocates and free tenant lawyers?A documented increase within the allowable cap, with correct notice and a current rent increase license, is the normal lawful practice. SF tenants have free counseling (Rent Board 415-252-4600) and free legal help (Eviction Defense Collaborative 415-659-9184); have a tenant lawyer review the notice and calculation before serving.SFGOV-ANNUAL-26 ↗ SFGOV-RENT-INC ↗ SFGOV-COUNSEL ↗ SFGOV-EVICTIONS ↗
Raise one unit's rent or both?Raise both units under the same uniform policy (same allowable percentage on each unit's base rent, same notice procedure).
