SAN FRANCISCO / OUTER SUNSET 94122 / RENT-CONTROLLED 1940 BUILDING

Law: local / state / federal

What each legal layer actually says about rent increases for this building.

Layer order: for this 1940 two-unit building the San Francisco Rent Ordinance controls the amount (1.6% cap). The statewide AB 1482 cap does not apply where stricter local rent control exists (Civ. Code 1947.12(d)(3)); the state notice statute and the federal Fair Housing Act still apply.
