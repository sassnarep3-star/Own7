Raise one unit's rent or both?Applying the same lawful policy to both units is the normal, defensible practice: the same allowable percentage, computed on each unit's own base rent, with the same notice procedure for each. The federal Fair Housing Act makes it unlawful to discriminate in the terms or conditions of a rental (including the amount of an increase) because of a protected class, so treating identical units differently without a neutral, documented, lawful reason (for example a different anniversary date) invites scrutiny. Raising only one unit is not automatically illegal, but uniform treatment of both units is the approach that passes review most cleanlyFHA-3604 ↗ SFGOV-RENT-INC ↗ SFGOV-BANKED ↗ HUD-FHEO ↗
