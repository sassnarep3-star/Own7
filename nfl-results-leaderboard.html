REWARDS

Why no reward exists

The brief asks for a leaderboard that rewards top strategies. The blocker is not the statistics, it is the settlement. A reward has to be paid out against something, and the only honest reference is a price that was available before kickoff. No sportsbook price was retrieved in this project, and NFL.com terms do not permit building a redistributable odds dataset.

So the format is frozen and the entries are real, but the reward column stays empty. When a licensed price feed exists, the same pipeline settles against it without changing any of the grading rules: the entry that clears every gate takes the points, and nothing else does.

What would change the answer: a licensed pre-kickoff price archive with documented redistribution rights. Until then, ROI and closing-line value are blank by rule, not by omission.
