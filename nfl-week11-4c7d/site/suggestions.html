REVIEW

How to use this page

Pick the highest-ranked suggestion whose adopt path you are willing to fund, write the decision into the repository, and the gates it unblocks open. Deferring is a valid answer and is recorded as such - the only destructive choice is the reject path, and each row says what it costs.
