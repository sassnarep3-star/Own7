RELIABILITY

Do the labels mean anything?

The brief asks for an empirical calibration of 'Questionable' instead of a legacy percentage. This is the honest version of that answer: a rate per designation, the sample size behind it, and a 95% Wilson interval that shows how far the truth could still move.

One season is not a calibration

Out and Questionable each hold 18 joined rows. The interval for Questionable runs from 0.1250 to 0.5087 - wide enough to contain both 'rarely' and 'about half'. Publishing a point estimate here would be an invention.

What would turn this into a calibration

Archived weekly injury reports for at least two further seasons, joined the same way. That is requirement R66 and it is the only route: no amount of extra current-week data narrows an interval that is limited by how many times a designation has been published at all.
