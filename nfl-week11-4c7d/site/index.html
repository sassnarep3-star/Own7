SCOPE

What this desk is

The brief asks for eight things before any number can be trusted: a price store, a verified results feed, three-stage origination, a fair-versus-market column, a leaderboard that separates signal from staking, replacement depth, correlations with n, and a demotion gate. Those live in the competition and readiness desks. This desk does the narrower job that was left undone on 2026-11-18: close Week 10 completely, prove the closure arithmetically, and say precisely which gates are still shut and what number would open each one.

Everything here was read from an official page on 2026-11-18 and is retained verbatim in evidence/ with a SHA-256. Nothing is inferred. Where an official source prints nothing, the cell is blank.
