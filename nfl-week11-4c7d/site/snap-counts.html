QUESTION

Is there an official snap-count source?

The brief asks twice for depth to be modelled from playing time rather than from jersey order, and once for snap counts to stand in when a club publishes no depth chart. All three requirements depend on one thing existing: an official source that publishes how much each player played. This desk read four official surfaces looking for it, and one more for the inactive-list question, and recorded exactly what each one rendered.

SUBSTITUTE

What we use instead, and what it cannot do

The official Game Book prints lineups, substitutions, Did Not Play and Not Active. That is official playing-participation evidence: it tells you who started and who was unavailable. It does not tell you how much anyone played, so it cannot distinguish a starter who played every snap from one who left after six. Until an official snap-count source is found, every playing-time requirement stays open - not for lack of method, but for lack of a source.

Explicitly rejected

Deriving depth order from jersey number, experience or salary was proposed earlier in this project and rejected in the brief. This desk rejects it again: none of those three is a measure of playing time.
