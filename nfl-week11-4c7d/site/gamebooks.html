CLOSURE

Week 10 is closed: 14 of 14 games

The previous capture held 13 of 14 games; the Monday-night game had not finished. It has now. Every game in the slate carries an official Game Book, and every Game Book prints the stadium, the city, the roof and surface description, the game weather, temperature, humidity, wind and paid attendance. Those are the fixture-context fields the brief asks for, and they come from the league's own document rather than from a third party.

Two games print no weather at all

Mercedes-Benz Stadium and Caesars Superdome leave the weather, temperature, humidity and wind cells empty in the official Game Book, while two other indoor games do print weather. Those four cells are blank on this page because the source printed nothing - not because the value is zero.

The weather is only official after the fact

These weather fields are printed in the post-game Game Book. A pre-kickoff forecast for the same venue is a different dataset and is not published on the surfaces this desk read, so no pre-game weather cell is filled in.

Three official statuses, not two

The Game Book separates 'Not Active' (deactivated) from 'Did Not Play' (dressed and available but unused). Conflating them would overstate the injury effect; this desk keeps them apart.
