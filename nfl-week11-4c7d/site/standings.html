Every club reconciles from the snapshot plus one result

The retained 2026-11-17 snapshot was taken before the Monday-night game. Adding that game's official score to the two clubs that played it - and to no one else - must reproduce the standings page read on 2026-11-18. It does, for all 32 clubs and all seven checked fields: wins, losses, ties, percentage, points for, points against and net points. The percentage is recomputed as (wins + half the ties) divided by games played and compared to the figure the page prints.

Why this matters

A results feed that reconciles to the official standings on every club field cannot be silently wrong about a game. It is the cheapest available proof that the corpus is complete.
