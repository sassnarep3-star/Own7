Method & limits

How the forward desk captures, checks and orders its work.

What this desk does

The forward desk takes a fresh official read of the standings, the Week 11 schedule and the Week 10 injury report; checks it mechanically against the committed canonical data; and joins the open decisions to what blocks them and the official source that would answer each one. It then scores the suggestion register so a reviewer can choose the best path before implementing any change.

Drift-check rule

A fresh read is compared cell-for-cell against every committed canonical snapshot. Contradictions abort the check; drift against a stale snapshot is reported as a finding and never patched into the canonical file. The canonical files are historical snapshots and are left as-is.

Suggestion rubric

Five criteria, each scored 0–3 with a written rationale: requirement coverage (how many non-implemented requirements a suggestion moves), decision leverage (Blocked > Partial > Open), source feasibility (does the missing info name an obtainable official source), dependency weight (does it share a decision link with other suggestions), and implementation risk (reuses registered sources = lower risk). Composite = coverage×3 + leverage×2 + source×2 + dependency×1 + risk×1.

What this desk never does

It publishes no price, stake, wager, return on investment or cash reward. It infers no value from a non-official source. Where a value cannot be confirmed from an official page, the cell is blank and flagged, never filled in.
