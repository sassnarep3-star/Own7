PROSPECTIVE BY DESIGN

The shadow ledger is open and empty

Open and empty.

  • One row per pre-kickoff entry: timestamp, game, market, line, price, operator and a screenshot-free text capture of the quote.
  • Stake capped at a fixed fraction of a notional bankroll; no chasing and no stake increase after a loss.
  • Score only after settlement using the operator's published rule.
  • Report closing-line value and the lower confidence bound, never a single hot run.
  • Every entry keeps its losing rows; deleting a row disqualifies the strategy.
