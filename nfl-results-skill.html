Skill vs luck

Which strategies beat an exact chance null, survive multiplicity control and hold up out of sample.

Research JSONResearch only
Read this before the tables

A strategy is called skill only if it beats an exact chance null, survives correction for testing many strategies, and holds up in both halves of the season. Most do not. That is the finding, not a failure of the analysis.

Hit rate here means the board's favoured side (relative to the league-average line) beat the result. Permutation p shuffles the real results 20,000 times and asks how often chance matches the observed record.

The asymmetry is the point. The same model, on the same data, produces a spread line that clears every testable gate and a total line that lands at exactly a coin flip. A total is far harder to beat from scoring rates alone because it depends on pace, turnovers and red-zone execution, none of which this model sees. Publishing only the spread result would be cherry-picking.

q is the Benjamini–Hochberg adjusted value across all 30 variants. At a 10% false-discovery ceiling only q ≤ 0.10 counts as surviving multiplicity. One entry clears it, and that entry is the board benchmark, not a challenger.

METHOD

How luck was removed

1. An exact chance null. For every record we compute the probability of doing at least that well if the strategy were flipping a fair coin. With 30 variants tested, small p-values appear by construction, so this alone is not enough.

2. Family-wise correction. Benjamini–Hochberg adjusts every p-value for the size of the family. The strongest challenger does not survive it.

3. A permutation test. The realised results are shuffled 20,000 times and the board is re-scored each time. The observed spread record is beaten by chance in 0.21% of shuffles — the null distribution's 95th percentile is 57.5% while the observation is 64.2%.

4. Split-half and leave-one-week-out. Both halves must beat 50%, and no single week may carry the result. Dropping any one week leaves the spread hit rate between 57.7% and 65.4%.

5. Sample size honesty. At the observed edge, roughly 75 settled bets are needed for 80% power. There are 120, so the record is adequate but not generous.
