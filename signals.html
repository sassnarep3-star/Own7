PRICING NOTES

From forecast to fair line

For a binary event with no push or void, fair decimal odds = 1 / forecast probability. At decimal odds d, expected net return per unit = p × d − 1. For push or void outcomes, use the full settlement distribution and the sportsbook’s actual rules instead.

For a two-leg parlay, the joint probability is P(A ∩ B); multiplying P(A) × P(B) assumes independence. Correlation alone does not identify the joint probability of arbitrary outcomes, and sportsbook prices and rules determine whether a combination is available.

These are mathematical definitions and proposed procedures, not sourced performance results. Do not treat hypothetical edges as executable bets.
