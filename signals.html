Opponent-adjusted marginS01Shrink team strength toward league mean; fit score margin using lagged offense/defense, home court and opponent strength.
Distribution of home margin; derive spread cover and moneyline from the same coherent score distribution.
Failure modes

Raw win percentage is NOT a matchup win probability. Full-season aggregates may seed a later season only, never earlier games.

Pace × efficiency totalS02Forecast possessions and both teams’ points per possession using prior-game data; allow their residuals to co-move.
μ_total = E[possessions × (PPP_home + PPP_away)]; integrate a calibrated distribution at the offered total.
Failure modes

PTS_home average + PTS_away average is not an opponent-adjusted total model. Overtime and pushes must match operator rules.

Update score/participation distributions at a fixed pregame cutoff; abstain when material availability is unknown.
Failure modes

A shared schedule factor does not prove independence or a profitable rest effect; do not use final minutes as a pregame feature.
