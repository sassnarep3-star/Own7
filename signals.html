Pricing specification · project design

For a no-push binary event with estimated probability p: fair decimal odds = 1/p; net expected value per unit = p × offered decimal odds − 1. These are mathematical definitions, not estimated NBA prices. With pushes: net EV = p(win) × (odds − 1) − p(loss); pushes return stake. For two-way, no-push markets, proportional no-vig baseline qᵢ = (1/dᵢ) / Σ(1/dⱼ); this is a chosen normalization, not proof of true market probabilities.

For parlays use P(all legs win), not a product of marginal probabilities unless independence is justified. Pairwise correlation alone does not identify a multi-leg joint tail. Settle voids, pushes, overtime and DNPs according to the exact operator rules before evaluation. No operator quotes or settlement rules have been verified here.
