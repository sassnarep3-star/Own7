What the project now does

Preserves source scope, verifies retained observations, calculates paired statistics, demonstrates chronological probability estimation, and rejects unsupported leaderboard entries. Each model family has a testable contract; every table row has an official input, method, or project-artifact link.

Pricing primitives, proper scores, multiple-testing adjustment, gate contracts and deterministic ranking are implemented and tested. The website builds without runtime dependencies, credentials or manual data entry.

Full requirements traceability →

What it does not establish

No skill-based wagering edge, actual historical pregame availability, operator permission, current offer, correctly priced multi-leg bet or future profit. The small retrospective study is not a substitute for independent forward validation.

Luck cannot be eliminated. Reward rules use non-monetary merit points only, and no strategy currently qualifies. Unresolved rights and data items are published rather than silently filled in.
