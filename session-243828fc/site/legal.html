Legal & responsible gambling

What the Singapore regulator and NFL.com say. Sourced, not advice.

Not legal advice. This page records what the official sources say. It does not decide whether any activity is lawful for any person. Before any leaderboard, reward, or wagering-linked use is designed or run from Singapore, a qualified Singapore lawyer should review it. This project does not recommend or place wagers.
TopicWhat the source saysSource noteSource (official)
Unlicensed remote gambling in or from SingaporeUnlawful for any person to provide unlicensed remote gambling services in or from Singapore, or from outside Singapore to persons situated in Singapore. Singapore Pools is the only operator the GRA has licensed to provide remote gambling services.Official GRA page, read 14 Nov 2026https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/unlawful-remote-gambling-activities
Participating in unlicensed remote gambling (individuals)The GRA page states it is an offence for any person in Singapore to participate in unlicensed remote gambling; on conviction a fine not exceeding $10,000, or up to 6 months' imprisonment, or both.Official GRA page, read 14 Nov 2026. Note: some secondary blogs quote $5,000; the GRA figure is used here.https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/unlawful-remote-gambling-activities
Penalties for operatorsUp to $500,000 fine and up to 7 years' imprisonment; repeat offenders up to $700,000 and 10 years, per the GRA page.Official GRA page, read 14 Nov 2026https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/unlawful-remote-gambling-activities
Reporting and enforcementFrom 1 Jan 2025 the Singapore Police Force handles blocking of unlawful remote gambling services, their advertising and payment transactions (GRA page).Official GRA page, read 14 Nov 2026https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/unlawful-remote-gambling-activities
Responsible-gambling resourcesGRA links to the National Council on Problem Gambling as part of Singapore's national framework for problem gambling.Official GRA page, read 14 Nov 2026https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/problem-gambling
