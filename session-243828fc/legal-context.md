# Singapore and NFL.com legal context (P4), 14 Nov 2026

Not legal advice. Full table: [legal.html](legal.html).

- GRA (official): unlicensed remote gambling in or from Singapore is unlawful; Singapore Pools is the only operator the GRA names as licensed for remote gambling. Source: https://www.gra.gov.sg/harm-minimisation/unlawful-remote-gambling-activities
- Penalties as stated on that GRA page: individuals who take part, up to $10,000 and/or up to 6 months; operators up to $500,000 and 7 years (repeat offenders $700,000 and 10 years).
- The Remote Gambling Act 2014 statute text could not be retrieved (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/RGA2014 returned "Page Not Found"). Statute wording is UNVERIFIED.
- NFL.com terms §1.3 (updated May 16, 2024): personal, non-commercial, informational use; systematic retrieval to compile a collection prohibited without written consent. Source: https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms
- Jurisdiction: the project brief names Singapore. No source here establishes where any particular user is located, so this page does not decide which law applies to a given person.

Result for this project: no wagering-linked design, reward, or bet is built or recommended until a Singapore lawyer reviews it (flag F08; suggestion S05).
